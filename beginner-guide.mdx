# Build a Base and Steal Beginner Guide - Complete First Session Walkthrough

Welcome to **Build a Base and Steal**, the ultimate Roblox tycoon simulation game where strategy meets creativity. This comprehensive beginner guide will walk you through your first session, explain core mechanics, and set you on the path to building impenetrable bases and stealing legendary pets from other players.

## Getting Started: Your First 15 Minutes

When you first enter **Build a Base and Steal**, you'll spawn in your own base area with starting cash. Here's exactly what to do during your first session:

### Step 1: Understand the Core Loop
The game revolves around three main activities:
- **Rolling for Pets**: Spend cash to get random pets with varying rarities
- **Building Defense**: Construct walls and traps to protect your pets
- **Raiding Others**: Use gear to break into enemy bases and steal their pets

### Step 2: Your First Rolls
Start by spending your initial cash on pet rolls. Don't worry about getting rare pets yet – focus on building a collection. Even common pets generate passive income, which is crucial for early game progression.

**Pro Tip**: Roll in batches of 5-10 to maximize your chances of getting better pets through the game's pity system.

### Step 3: Set Up Basic Defense
Once you have a few pets, immediately build basic walls around your base. New players often make the mistake of focusing only on pets, but unprotected pets are easy targets for raiders.

## Understanding Pet Rolling Mechanics

### The Rolling System
Pet rolling in **Build a Base and Steal** operates on a gacha-style system with dynamic costs and rarities:

- **Common Pets**: 60% drop rate, low income generation
- **Uncommon Pets**: 25% drop rate, moderate income  
- **Rare Pets**: 10% drop rate, high income
- **Legendary Pets**: 4% drop rate, very high income
- **Mythic Pets**: 1% drop rate, maximum income

### Budget Planning for Beginners
Never spend all your cash on rolling! Follow this golden rule:
- **70%** of your cash for rolling
- **20%** for base defense and upgrades
- **10%** emergency reserve for raiding gear

This allocation ensures you can always protect what you earn and have options when opportunities arise.

## Passive Income: The Key to Success

### How Offline Money Works
One of **Build a Base and Steal's** most attractive features is its offline income system. Your pets continue generating cash even when you're not playing:

- **Income Rate**: Calculated based on pet rarity and quantity
- **Offline Cap**: Maximum earnings limited to prevent exploitation
- **Collection**: Claim accumulated earnings when you return

### Maximizing Offline Income
To optimize passive income:
1. **Balance Quality and Quantity**: More pets = more income, even if they're common
2. **Upgrade Pet Storage**: Unlock more slots to hold additional income generators
3. **Time Your Logouts**: Log off after major upgrades to maximize accumulation

## Base Building Fundamentals

### Your First Base Layout
Start with a simple but effective design:
- **Central Chamber**: Place your most valuable pets here
- **Outer Perimeter**: Multiple layers of walls create depth
- **Trap Placement**: Position traps at chokepoints and corners
- **Access Points**: Limit entrances to control raiding paths

### Essential Building Materials
As a beginner, focus on these core defensive elements:
- **Basic Walls**: Your primary defense barrier
- **Reinforced Walls**: Upgrade priority for high-traffic areas
- **Floor Traps**: Damage raiders walking over them
- **Turrets**: Automatic defense that targets intruders

## Preparing for Your First Raid

### Gear Basics
Before attempting raids, understand the gear system:
- **Hammers**: Break through walls and barriers
- **Lockpicks**: Access locked areas faster
- **Speed Boosts**: Move quickly through enemy bases
- **Health Potions**: Survive defensive traps and turrets

### Raid Etiquette and Strategy
Smart raiding is about efficiency, not brute force:
1. **Scout First**: Observe base layouts before attacking
2. **Target Wealthy Bases**: Look for players with valuable pet collections
3. **Time Your Raids**: Strike when owners are likely offline
4. **Escape Planning**: Know your exit routes before entering

## Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Neglecting Defense
The most common error new players make is focusing entirely on pet collection while leaving their base vulnerable. Remember: a stolen pet generates zero income for you.

### 2. Poor Cash Management
Spending everything on rolling leaves you defenseless and unable to raid. Always maintain your emergency reserve.

### 3. Ignoring the Community
**Build a Base and Steal** has an active community. Join the Discord, watch YouTube guides, and learn from experienced players.

### 4. Rushing Advanced Content
Don't try to raid high-level players immediately. Build your foundation first, then progressively challenge tougher targets.
