### Long-Term Planning Considerations

Thinking about the discord servers aspects of Build a Base and Steal on a quarterly or monthly horizon is what separates casual players from players who climb the leaderboard. This section covers the long-term planning considerations that pay off over weeks and months, not days.

**Plan one: the patch watch list.** Track which parts of the game are most likely to change in the next patch, and have a backup strategy ready. The watch list helps you avoid over-investing in a system that is about to be rebalanced, and it gives you advance warning to shift your rolling and trading focus before the patch lands.

**Plan two: the friends network.** Build a small network of trusted players who you can trade with, share information with, and occasionally coordinate with for events and raids. The network does not need to be large; 5 to 10 active contacts is enough to provide most of the benefits. The network compounds: the longer you maintain it, the more valuable it becomes.

A rotation of 3 to 5 targets gives you variety without requiring you to learn a new base every session. The familiarity compounds: the more you raid a particular base layout, the faster and more profitable your raids become.

**Plan four: the weekly review.** Spend 15 minutes at the end of each week reviewing your progress, your pet collection, your base, and your trades. The review helps you spot trends that are not visible in a single session, and it gives you a chance to adjust your strategy before small problems become big ones.
