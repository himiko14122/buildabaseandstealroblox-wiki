## Tier List Controversies

The Build a Base and Steal community often disagrees on tier placements. Common controversies include:

- **Accessibility**: Should rare but powerful items be ranked higher than common but weaker items?

- **Playstyle Dependence**: Should items that are only good for specific playstyles be ranked highly?

### Respecting Different Perspectives
Different players have different experiences and priorities. A tier list that's perfect for one player may be wrong for another. Respect different perspectives and understand that there's no single "correct" tier list.

### Evolving Meta
The meta is constantly shifting with updates and balance changes. Tier lists that were accurate last month may be outdated this month. Always check the update date of any tier list you reference.

Tier lists are valuable tools for understanding the Build a Base and Steal meta, but they're not absolute truths. Use them as guides, not rules, and adapt them to your own playstyle and experience.
