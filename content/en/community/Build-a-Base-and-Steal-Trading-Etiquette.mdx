## Core Trading Principles

### Honesty and Transparency
Always be honest about the items you're trading. Disclose the exact stats, friendship level, and any flaws or issues with the item. Misrepresenting an item is considered scamming and will damage your reputation permanently.

**Example**: If you're selling a legendary pet with a weak special ability, clearly state this upfront. Don't let the buyer discover it after the trade.

### Fair Pricing
Price your items fairly based on current market conditions. Check community price guides and recent sales to determine appropriate prices. Overcharging new players who don't know better is unethical.

**Guideline**: Price within 10-20% of the community-established price range. Prices significantly outside this range are suspicious.

### Honor Agreements
Once you agree to a trade, honor it. Backing out of a confirmed trade without a good reason is unprofessional and will hurt your reputation. Only back out if you discover a legitimate issue with the item.

**Exception**: If you discover the item is bugged or not as described, you have a valid reason to back out. Explain the situation to the other party.

## Pre-Trade Etiquette

### Clear Communication
Communicate clearly about what you're offering and what you're seeking. Use specific details rather than vague descriptions. Ambiguity leads to misunderstandings and bad trading experiences.

**Good**: "Selling level 10 legendary Eternal Phoenix with 85% friendship, 7,500 cash firm."
**Bad**: "Selling legendary phoenix, make offer."

### Patience
Don't rush other players into decisions. Give them time to consider the trade, check their resources, and respond. Pressuring players creates a negative experience and may lead to regretted trades.

### Verification
Before finalizing a trade, verify that both parties understand the terms. Confirm the item details, the price, and any additional conditions. This final confirmation prevents misunderstandings.

## During the Trade

### Use Official Trading Interface
Always use the official Build a Base and Steal trading interface. Never agree to trades outside the official system, as these are almost always scams. The official interface provides protection for both parties.

### Middlemen for High-Value Trades
For high-value trades (10,000+ cash), consider using a trusted community middleman. Middlemen hold items from both parties and ensure both sides receive their agreed-upon items. This protects both parties from scams.

**Finding a Middleman**: Ask in the trading Discord channel for recommendations. The community maintains a list of trusted middlemen.

### Double-Check Everything
Before confirming the trade, double-check that you're trading the correct items and receiving the correct items. Once the trade is confirmed, it cannot be reversed. Take your time and verify everything carefully.

## Post-Trade Etiquette

### Leave Feedback
If the trading platform supports it, leave feedback for your trading partner. Positive feedback helps build good reputations, while negative feedback warns the community about bad traders.

### Thank Your Partner
Send a brief thank you message after a successful trade. This simple gesture builds goodwill and encourages positive trading experiences.

### Report Scammers
If you encounter a scammer, report them to the moderators with evidence. Include screenshots of the conversation and the trade details. Reporting scammers protects the community from future bad experiences.

## Avoiding Common Scams

### The Switch Scam
Scammers may agree to trade one item but switch it for a similar-looking lower-value item at the last moment.

**Prevention**: Always verify the item details in the trading interface before confirming. Pay attention to item names, rarities, and stats.

### The Price Switch Scam
Scammers may agree to one price but demand more at the last moment, claiming the item is more valuable than previously discussed.

**Prevention**: Confirm the final price before initiating the trade and refuse to pay more than the agreed amount.

### The Fake Middleman Scam
Scammers may pretend to be a trusted middleman and run off with both parties' items.

**Prevention**: Only use middlemen recommended by the community. Verify the middleman's identity through official channels.

### The "Pay First" Scam
Scammers may ask you to pay first, promising to deliver the item later. They then disappear with your payment.

**Prevention**: Never pay outside the official trading interface. Always use the system that protects both parties simultaneously.

## Building a Positive Reputation

### Consistency
Consistently conduct fair trades over time to build a positive reputation. One good trade doesn't make a reputation, but dozens of good trades do.

### Community Involvement
Participate in the trading community beyond just making trades. Share market insights, help new players understand pricing, and contribute to price guides.

### Conflict Resolution
If a trade goes wrong, communicate respectfully to resolve the issue. Most disputes can be resolved through calm discussion. Escalate to moderators only if necessary.

### Transparency
Be transparent about your trading history and reputation. If you've made mistakes in the past, acknowledge them and show how you've improved. Honesty about past issues builds trust.

## Trading with New Players

### Education
When trading with new players, take time to educate them about fair prices and trading etiquette. Explain the community price guide and help them understand the value of their items.

### Fairness
Avoid taking advantage of new players who don't know market prices. Offer fair prices even if you could get away with charging more. Building a positive reputation is worth more than a quick profit.

### Patience
New players may have questions or need time to understand the trading process. Be patient and helpful, not frustrated or dismissive.

## Consequences of Bad Etiquette

### Reputation Damage
Bad trading etiquette leads to a damaged reputation. The Build a Base and Steal community is relatively small, and news of bad traders spreads quickly. A damaged reputation makes it difficult to find trading partners.

### Community Exclusion
Repeated bad etiquette can lead to exclusion from trading communities and Discord servers. Moderators may ban traders with a history of scams or unethical behavior.

### Account Action
In severe cases, developers may take action against accounts that repeatedly scam or exploit other players. This can range from temporary bans to permanent account termination.

Good trading etiquette benefits everyone. It creates a positive community, reduces scams, and makes trading more enjoyable for all participants. Follow these guidelines to build a positive reputation and contribute to a healthy trading environment.
