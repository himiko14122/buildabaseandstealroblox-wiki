### Long-Term Planning Considerations

Thinking about the youtube creators aspects of Build a Base and Steal on a quarterly or monthly horizon is what separates casual players from players who climb the leaderboard. This section covers the long-term planning considerations that pay off over weeks and months, not days.

**Plan one: the raid rotation.** Identify a small set of raid targets that you can rotate through, and refine your approach to each one. A rotation of 3 to 5 targets gives you variety without requiring you to learn a new base every session. The familiarity compounds: the more you raid a particular base layout, the faster and more profitable your raids become.

**Plan two: the weekly review.** Spend 15 minutes at the end of each week reviewing your progress, your pet collection, your base, and your trades. The review helps you spot trends that are not visible in a single session, and it gives you a chance to adjust your strategy before small problems become big ones. The players who do weekly reviews consistently out-progress the players who don't.

**Plan three: the rolling calendar.** Map out which pets you want to target each month, and align your rolling budget with events that boost the drop rates of those pets. The calendar does not need to be perfect, but it should give you a direction so that every roll is a deliberate choice, not a random impulse.

**Plan four: the trading reserve.** Keep a small reserve of high-value pets in storage for trading purposes, even if you would rather use them in your own base. The reserve gives you flexibility to make trades that improve your collection without spending rolling budget. The reserve should be roughly 10 to 20 percent of your best pets, kept aside specifically for trading.
