## Major Community Events

### Build a Base and Steal World Championship
**Frequency**: Annual (November)
**Format**: Multi-stage tournament with qualifying rounds
**Prize Pool**: $10,000 + exclusive in-game rewards
**Participants**: Top 100 players from regional qualifiers

The World Championship is the premier competitive event in Build a Base and Steal. Players compete in raiding, base building, and strategy challenges to determine the best player in the world.

**Event Structure**:
- Regional Qualifiers (October): Players compete in their regions for championship spots

- Group Stage (November 1-7): 100 players divided into groups, top 2 advance

- Knockout Stage (November 8-14): Single-elimination bracket

- Grand Finals (November 15): Top 2 players compete for the championship

### Monthly Speedrunning Tournament
**Frequency**: Monthly (Last weekend)
**Format**: Time-attack competition on specific base types
**Prize Pool**: 5,000,000 cash + exclusive titles
**Participants**: Open to all players

The Monthly Speedrunning Tournament challenges players to complete raids as quickly as possible. Each month features different base types and rules to keep the competition fresh.

**Categories**:
- Any%: Complete the raid by any means

- 100%: Collect all pets and destroy all walls

- No-Damage: Complete without taking any damage

- Restricted: Complete with specific gear restrictions

### Faction Wars League
**Frequency**: Quarterly
**Format**: Team-based competition between factions
**Prize Pool**: Faction reputation + exclusive faction pets
**Participants**: All faction members

The Faction Wars League pits factions against each other in territorial control battles. Factions earn points for controlling territories, raiding enemy bases, and defending their own bases.

**Season Structure**:
- Pre-season (Week 1): Practice and coordination

- Regular season (Weeks 2-8): Weekly faction battles

- Playoffs (Weeks 9-10): Top factions compete for the championship

- Championship (Week 11): Final battle for supremacy

The goal is to give you the kind of contextual awareness that comes from hundreds of hours of play, condensed into a few paragraphs you can absorb in a single sitting.

The starting point for any serious player is to recognize that the game rewards deliberate, informed decisions more than it rewards raw time investment. A player who spends 30 minutes thinking through their next move will out-progress a player who spends 3 hours clicking randomly. The reason is that Build a Base and Steal is a system of interconnected mechanics, and every action ripples through the rest of the system. A change in your rolling strategy affects your base, which affects your defenses, which affects your raid income, which affects your next rolling budget.

The second thing is that community has a feedback loop with the rest of the game. A stronger base leads to more successful defenses, which leads to more retained pets, which leads to higher offline income, which leads to faster progression, which leads to a stronger base. The opposite feedback loop is also true: a weak base leads to lost pets, slower progression, and weaker defenses. The early-game decisions have outsized impact because they determine which loop you enter.

Finally, community rewards consistency over intensity. The player who logs in for 30 minutes every day and follows a deliberate plan will out-progress the player who logs in for 5 hours once a week and does whatever catches their attention. The reason is the same as for any compounding system: small, regular actions compound into large gains over time, while sporadic large actions produce sporadic large results with no compounding.



### Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

New players in the community events area of Build a Base and Steal tend to make a predictable set of mistakes, and most of these mistakes are not obvious until you have made them a few times. This section lists the most common pitfalls and explains how to avoid each one.

**Pitfall one: ignoring the trading economy.** The temptation is to trades can give you access to pets you cannot easily roll. The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Build a small reserve of tradeable pets and learn to read the market. Even a few successful trades can fund weeks of rolling.

**Pitfall two: over-investing in a single base design.** The temptation is to base that worked in the early game will not work in the mid game. The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Plan for base evolution from the start, and design walls and traps that can be upgraded incrementally without rebuilding from scratch.

**Pitfall three: ignoring offline income.** The temptation is to largest source of cash for most players, and it is the one that beginners most often neglect. The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Set up your base for maximum offline accumulation, position your best pets in the core vault, and log off deliberately when your base is in its strongest state.

**Pitfall four: chasing the meta pet instead of building a collection.** The temptation is to current S tier pet looks great on paper, but chasing a single S tier pet is expensive. The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Build a collection of A and B tier pets that provide reliable income and good family bonuses, then use the income to roll for S tier pets when events boost their drop rates.
