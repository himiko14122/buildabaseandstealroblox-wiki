## Getting Started with Trading

Trading in Build a Base and Steal allows players to exchange items, pets, and currency with other players. Before diving into trades, understand the fundamental mechanics:

## Safe Trading Practices

### Pre-Trade Checklist
Before finalizing any trade:
- **Verify Identity**: Confirm you're trading with the right player

- **Check Reputation**: Ask about their trading history

- **Review Terms**: Clearly state what each party receives

- **Secure Location**: Trade in safe, neutral areas

- **Witness Presence**: Have trusted players observe high-value trades

### Avoiding Common Scams
Protect yourself from known trading scams:
- **Item Switching**: Scammers may swap items at the last moment

- **Fake Offers**: Too-good-to-be-true deals often hide catches

- **Multi-Trade Complexity**: Overly complex trades can hide scams

- **Rush Tactics**: Urgency often signals something wrong

- **External Payment**: Never trade for real money or external items

If something feels suspicious, it's better to pass on the trade than risk losing valuable items.
