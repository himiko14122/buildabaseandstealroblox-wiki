## Advanced Raiding Techniques

### Stealth Approaches

**Stealth raiding** minimizes detection and defensive response, allowing you to complete theft attempts before base owners can react. This approach emphasizes **silent entry methods**, avoiding trigger mechanisms, and minimizing the time spent inside the target base. Use lock picking tools instead of breaching equipment to enter through doors without creating noise. Move carefully through the base avoiding pressure plates and motion sensors that could trigger defensive systems. Plan your route in advance to minimize the distance traveled and reduce the chances of accidental detection.

**Distraction techniques** can enhance stealth approaches by drawing defensive attention away from your actual target. Create **diversionary activities** on one side of the base while you approach valuable assets from another direction. Use decoy objects or noise-making items to trigger defensive systems in controlled locations, allowing you to bypass more challenging obstacles. The timing and coordination of distractions requires practice but can significantly improve your success rate against well-defended bases.

### Brute Force Strategies

**Direct assault approaches** rely on overwhelming force rather than stealth, using superior equipment and resources to quickly breach defenses and complete raids before effective countermeasures can be deployed. This strategy requires substantial investment in **high-damage breaching equipment** such as explosive charges and heavy hammers capable of rapidly destroying walls and doors. Speed is critical – the longer you spend engaged in direct confrontation with defensive systems, the more likely you are to trigger increasingly severe countermeasures that can jeopardize the entire raid.

**Coordination and timing** optimization enhances brute force effectiveness. Plan your attack sequence to maximize the impact of each breaching action, using explosive charges to create entry points and following immediately with rapid movement to target valuable assets before defensive systems can respond. Consider **team raiding** strategies where multiple players coordinate their attacks, with some creating distractions while others focus on theft objectives. This cooperative approach can overcome particularly challenging defenses that would be difficult to breach alone.
