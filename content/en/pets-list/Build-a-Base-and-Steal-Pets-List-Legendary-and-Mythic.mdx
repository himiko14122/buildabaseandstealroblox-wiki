## The Legendary Tier

The legendary tier contains roughly 15 to 20 individual pets, distributed across 4 to 6 families. Each family has 3 to 5 related pets, and each family has a collection bonus that activates when the full set is completed. Legendary pets generate 5x to 8x the base income rate of common pets, depending on the specific pet and the active family bonus. A complete legendary family can generate more income than the rest of a player's collection combined, which is why legendary pets are the foundation of the end-game economy.

The drop rate for legendary pets is roughly 0.5 to 1.5 percent, meaning that a player will receive a legendary pet every 70 to 200 rolls on average. The pity system guarantees a legendary within 250 rolls, which provides a hard upper bound on the cost of a guaranteed legendary. At the standard roll price, a guaranteed legendary requires a budget of 250 rolls, which is a substantial investment that most players cannot afford without months of saving.

### Top Legendary Families

The top legendary families in Build a Base and Steal are the most valuable collections in the entire game. The Celestial Dragons family, the Eternal Phoenix family, and the Cosmic Leviathan family are typical top performers, with collection bonuses that boost the entire family by 2x to 3x when completed. These families are also visually spectacular, with elaborate animations and unique effects that make them desirable to collectors and traders alike.

A complete legendary family is the ultimate achievement in Build a Base and Steal. Most end-game players have one or two legendary pets, and only the most dedicated players have a complete legendary family. The income from a complete legendary family is enough to fund a massive base defense, multiple legendary pet chases, and a steady stream of high-value

Legendary pets have the highest trading value of any pet tier, with prices typically ranging from 500x to 2000x the cost of a single roll. The exact price depends on the specific pet, its family, the current meta, and the activity level of the trading market. A legendary pet from a top family might sell for an amount that takes a casual player months of play to earn, which is why legendary pet trading is reserved for the most experienced players.

The trading market for legendary pets is thin and volatile. Most players cannot afford to buy a legendary pet outright, and the trades that do occur are usually complex multi-pet packages with significant cash adjustments. The trader who wants to participate in the legendary pet market needs a deep understanding of the meta, a substantial cash reserve, and a network of reliable trade partners. Legendary pet trading is not for the faint of heart, but the rewards can be enormous.

## The Mythic Tier

The mythic tier is the absolute apex of Build a Base and Steal's collection system. It contains a small number of pets, usually 5 to 10 individual pets, distributed across 2 to 3 families. Mythic pets generate 10x to 20x the base income rate of common pets, and their collection bonuses are so powerful that they can fundamentally change a player's income trajectory. A single mythic pet is a major achievement; a complete mythic family is something that fewer than 1 percent of players will ever achieve.

The drop rate for mythic pets is less than 0.5 percent, meaning that a player will receive a mythic pet every 200 to 500 rolls on average. The pity system for mythic pets is more complex than coordinated effort across multiple events.

### Top Mythic Families

The top mythic families in Build a Base and Steal are the most exclusive collections in the game. The Primordial Titans family, the Astral Guardians family, and the Void Sovereigns family are typical top performers, with collection bonuses that boost the entire family by 3x to 5x when completed. These families are visually stunning, with elaborate animations and unique effects that are unmatched by any other tier.

A complete mythic family is the single most prestigious achievement in Build a Base and Steal. Players who have completed a mythic family are typically the most dedicated, the most skilled, and the most resourceful members of the community. They are also usually the wealthiest, because the income from a complete mythic family is enough to fund anything else they want to do in the game.

### Mythic Pet Trading Value

Mythic pets have astronomical trading value, with prices that can exceed 10,000x the cost of a single roll. ## Strategies for Legendary and Mythic Pets

The optimal strategy for legendary and mythic pets depends on your current progression stage, your cash reserves, and your long-term goals. For most players, the priority should be to acquire at least one legendary pet, because the income boost from a single legendary is enough to accelerate the rest of the progression significantly. The specific legendary pet does not matter much at this stage; the goal is to have a legendary in the collection, not to have the best possible legendary.

For players who already have one or two legendaries, the priority should shift to family completion. Pick one legendary family to focus on, and roll specifically for the missing pieces until the family is complete. The income from a complete legendary family is substantially higher than the income from multiple partial families, which is why family completion is the most efficient use of the rolling budget for end-game players.

For players who already have a complete legendary family, the priority should be to start working on a mythic family. This is a long-term project that requires months of planning and saving, and most players will never reach this stage. But for the players who do, the rewards are the best that the game has to offer.

## Common Mistakes with End-Game Pets

The most common mistake with legendary and mythic pets is over-rolling for a specific pet that is not worth the cost. Legendary pet chases can easily consume a player's entire reserve, and the most common outcome of a legendary pet chase is a depleted reserve and no legendary to show for it. The fix is to set a per-pet rolling budget, to use the pity system to your advantage, and to walk away if the budget is exhausted without them through rolling. The player who learns to negotiate with experienced traders and to identify good deals in the market can acquire legendary and mythic pets more efficiently than the player who relies on rolling alone.

The third most common mistake is neglecting the rest of the collection in pursuit of a single legendary or mythic pet. A player with a single legendary and a weak base is in a much worse position than a player with a complete rare family, a strong base, and no legendaries. The legendary pet is a long-term goal, and chasing it at the expense of the rest of the game is almost always a mistake.
