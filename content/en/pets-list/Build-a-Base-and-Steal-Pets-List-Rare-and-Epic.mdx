## Strategies for Rare and Epic Pets

The optimal strategy for rare and epic pets depends on your current progression stage and your goals. For new players, the priority should be to acquire at least one rare pet and one epic pet as soon as possible, because the income boost from these pets is enough to accelerate the rest of the progression. The specific pet does not matter much at this stage; the goal is to have a rare and an epic in the collection, not to have the best possible rare and epic.

For intermediate players, the priority should shift to family completion. Pick one rare family and one epic family to focus on, and roll specifically for those families until they are complete. The income from a complete family is much higher than the income from a partial collection of the strongest individual pets, which is why family completion is the most efficient use of the rolling budget.

For advanced players, the priority should be to maintain a balanced collection across all rarities. A player with a complete epic family, two complete rare families, and a strong legendary collection is in a much better position than a player who missing pieces, rather than relying entirely on rolling.

## Common Mistakes with Mid-Tier Pets

The most common mistake with rare and epic pets is over-rolling for a specific pet that is not worth the cost. Some players fall in love with a particular pet and spend their entire rolling budget trying to obtain it, only to realize later that the pet is not as valuable as they thought. The fix is to set a per-pet rolling budget and to walk away if the budget is exhausted without success.

The second most common mistake is ignoring the trading market. Some players roll exclusively for rare and epic pets, when they could acquire the same pets more cheaply through trading. The market has prices for every rare and epic pet, and the player who learns to read the market can usually find better deals than the player who relies on rolling alone.

The third most common mistake is selling valuable family pieces during a panic. A player who needs cash for a wall upgrade or a gear purchase might sell a rare pet from a top family for a discount, not realizing that the same pet is worth much more to a player who is trying to complete that family. Before you sell any rare or epic pet, check the market to see what the pet is actually worth, and consider whether the cash you need is really worth the long-term value you are giving up.
