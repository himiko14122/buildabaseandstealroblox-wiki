The collection bonus system is one of the most important and most misunderstood features of Build a Base and Steal. While individual pets generate income on their own, the real income boost comes from completing pet families and unlocking their collection bonuses. A player with a partial collection of random pets will generate significantly less income than a player with a focused collection of completed families, even if the random collection includes more total pets. This guide explains exactly how the collection bonus system works, which bonuses are worth chasing, and how to prioritize your collection for maximum income.

## What Are Collection Bonuses

A collection bonus is a permanent income multiplier or special effect that activates when a player completes all the pets in a particular family. Each family in Build a Base and Steal has its own collection bonus, and the bonus is described in the family's information panel. The bonus is applied to every pet in the family, and it is permanent once the family is completed. Collection bonuses do not expire, and they are not lost when pets are stored or traded away (although the family will be marked as incomplete if any member is missing).

Collection bonuses come in several types. The most the family is complete, which is a substantial boost for a fully completed family. The second type is a tiered bonus, which increases in power as more pets from the family are collected. A tiered bonus might give 1.1x at 2 pets, 1.2x at 3 pets, and 1.3x at 4 pets, encouraging players to complete the family step by step. The third type is a unique effect, which provides a special ability that is not just an income boost. These effects are rare and valuable, and they are usually only available in the legendary and mythic tiers.

### How Bonuses Stack

Collection bonuses stack with each other and with the base income rate of the pets. A pet in a completed family with a 1.3x bonus will generate 1.3 times the pet's base income rate, and that 1.3x will be added to the player's other bonuses from other completed families. A player with three completed families, each with 1.3x bonuses, will have an effective income rate that is 1.3 times the sum of the base rates of all three families, which can be a substantial increase.

The bonuses also stack with the global bonuses that come from events, codes, and other sources. A player who has activated the 1.3x family bonus, the 1.1x event bonus, and the 1.05x code bonus will have an effective income rate that is 1.3 times 1.1 times 1.05, or about 1.5 times the base rate. This stacking is one of the reasons why Build a Base and Steal's order in which you complete families matters. Completing a high-bonus family first will give you a larger income boost than completing a low-bonus family first, and the boosted income will help you afford the next family more quickly. This compounding effect is why experienced players prioritize the highest-bonus families first, rather than completing families in the order of their rarity tier.

### The Best Collection Bonuses in the Game

The best collection bonuses in Build a Base and Steal are the ones that provide the largest income multipliers or the most useful unique effects. These bonuses are usually found in the legendary and mythic tiers, but there are some standout bonuses in the rare and epic tiers that are worth pursuing.

The top income bonus in the game is the Astral Guardians family bonus, which provides a 5x multiplier to all pets in the family when the full set is completed. The Astral Guardians are a mythic family with 5 pets, and completing the family is a long-term project that requires significant investment. But the payoff is enormous; a complete Astral Guardians family generates more income than most players' entire collections.

A second standout is the Celestial Dragons family bonus, which provides a 3x multiplier and a unique effect that boosts the income of all other legendary pets in the collection. This cross-family bonus is particularly valuable because it amplifies the rest of the player's collection, not just the family itself. A player with a complete Celestial Dragons family and several other legendary pets will.5x multiplier and a small chance to double the income of any individual pet at random. This random doubling effect is small in absolute terms but adds up over time, and the Phoenix Flock family is widely considered the best rare family to complete. The Epic tier has similar standout bonuses in the Cosmic Cats and Crystal Creatures families.

### Prioritizing Your Collection

The optimal collection priority depends on your current progression stage and your goals. For new players, the priority should be to complete one or two common or uncommon families, because the collection bonuses will provide a meaningful income boost without requiring a substantial rolling investment. The Puppy Pack, Kitten Clan, and Bunny Burrow families are good early-game targets.

For intermediate players, the priority should shift to a single rare family and a single epic family. The rare family should be one with a strong collection bonus, and the epic family should be one that is achievable within a few months of focused rolling. The Dragon Den, Phoenix Flock, and Fox Friends families are typical intermediate targets.

For advanced players, the priority should be a complete legendary family and the start of a mythic family. The legendary family should be one with the highest available collection bonus, and the mythic family should be the one that the player is closest to completing. The Celestial Dragons, Eternal Phoenix, and Cosmic Leviathan families are typical advanced targets.

## Common Mistakes with Collection Bonuses

The most common mistake is to chase individual pets without thinking about the family bonus. A player who rolls 200 times and ends up with 5 random legendary pets from 5 different families will have a much lower income than a player who rolls 200 times and ends up with a complete family of 5 sell pets that are part of an incomplete family. A player who has 4 of 5 pets in a family might sell the 5th pet they receive because they need cash for a wall upgrade, not realizing that selling the pet will break the family collection (or prevent it from being completed). The cash from the sale is usually less than the long-term value of completing the family, especially for high-bonus families.

The third most common mistake is to ignore the cross-family bonuses. Some family bonuses provide effects that boost other pets in the collection, not just the family itself. A player who completes a family with a cross-family bonus will see a much larger income boost than a player who completes a family with a self-contained bonus. Always check the family information panel for cross-family effects, and prioritize those families if you have other pets that would benefit from the boost.

## Long-Term Collection Planning

A long-term collection plan in Build a Base and Steal is a document that lists every family you intend to complete, the order in which you intend to complete them, and the rolling budget required for each. Most experienced players have a written plan that they refer to before each rolling session, and they adjust the plan as the meta shifts and as their priorities change.

The plan should be flexible. The meta will change, your goals will evolve, and unexpected opportunities will arise. A good collection plan is a guide, not a contract on the latest patch notes and the current state of your collection.

The plan should also be realistic. Trying to complete a mythic family in your first month of play is not realistic, and setting that as a goal will lead to frustration. The plan should have milestones that are achievable in the short term, with longer-term goals that stretch out over the year. The milestones should be celebrated, and the longer-term goals should be adjusted as you progress.

Finally, remember that the collection is a means to an end, not the end itself. The point of completing families and unlocking bonuses is to fund the parts of the game that you enjoy, whether that is base building, raiding, trading, or just the satisfaction of having a complete collection. Keep your goals aligned with your enjoyment, and the collection will become a source of satisfaction rather than a source of stress.
