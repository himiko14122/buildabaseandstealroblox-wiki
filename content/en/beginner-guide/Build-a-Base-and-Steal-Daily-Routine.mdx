A consistent daily routine is the single biggest difference between players who progress steadily in Build a Base and Steal and players who feel stuck despite playing regularly. The game rewards players who show up every day, claim their rewards, perform their core activities, and log off in a deliberate state. Players who log in randomly, do whatever catches their attention, and log off whenever they get bored tend to miss rewards, leave defenses unfinished, and accumulate inefficiencies that compound over time. This guide walks through the optimal daily routine from the moment you log in to the moment you log off, with a focus on the order of operations that maximizes your progression per minute played.

## The Five Phases of a Daily Session

A productive Build a Base and Steal session has five phases: claim, assess, act, optimize, and conclude. The claim phase is when you collect everything that has accumulated since your last session, including offline income, daily login rewards, event rewards, and any unclaimed gifts. The assess phase is when you review your current state and decide what to focus on for this session. The act phase is when you perform your core activities, like rolling, building, or raiding. The optimize phase is when you make small improvements to your base, your loadout, or your collection. The conclude phase is when you set up your base for offline accumulation and log off deliberately.

The order matters. Beginners who try to roll before claiming miss the bonus cash from login rewards, which means they have less to roll with. Beginners who skip the assess phase and jump straight to acting often work on the wrong priority, like rolling when they should be reinforcing a weak wall. Beginners who skip the optimize phase and log off immediately miss the small improvements that compound into a much stronger base over weeks. The five phases take maybe five minutes total to set up, and they ensure that every session is productive.

### Phase 1: Claim Everything

The first 30 to 60 seconds of every session should be dedicated to claiming rewards. The offline income is the largest reward, and it is the one most often missed by players who log in and immediately start playing. The offline income appears as a popup the moment you spawn, and you must click "claim" to collect it. If you do not click claim, the cash sits in the unclaimed state and is lost when the game calculates the next offline window.

After claiming offline income, claim the daily login reward. The daily login reward appears as a separate popup, and it usually grants a small amount of cash, a free roll, or a small item. The daily login streak bonus increases the reward amount, so logging in every day is more valuable than logging in occasionally, even if the daily sessions are short.

After the daily login, claim any unclaimed event rewards, code rewards, or mailbox gifts. These are usually visible as a small icon in the corner of the HUD, and they accumulate if you do not check them regularly. Some events grant rewards for completion, and the completion timer continues whether or not you are online, so logging in every day ensures you never miss a free reward.

### Phase 2: Assess Your State

The assess phase takes 1 to 2 minutes and is where you decide what to focus on during the session. The assessment has three parts: review your pet collection, review your base, and review any active events.

Reviewing your pet collection means opening the collection menu and checking the storage indicator, the family bonuses you are close to completing, and any rare pets you have rolled since your last session. The assessment should be quick: you are looking for actionable information, not doing a deep audit. If you are one pet away from completing a family bonus, that is a rolling priority. If your storage is over 80 percent full, that is a recycling priority. If you rolled a legendary since your last session, that is a protection priority.

Reviewing your base means walking the perimeter and looking for breaches, weak walls, or missing traps. If your base was raided while you were offline, the raid interface will tell you which walls were damaged and which pets were stolen. The base review is the time to plan repairs and reinforcements. If your base is in good shape, the review is short, and you can move on to the act phase.

Reviewing active events means checking the event panel for any limited-time activities that you should prioritize. Some events grant bonus rewards for specific actions, like "roll 50 times today for a free legendary ticket." If such an event is active, the act phase should be planned around it. If no events are active, the act phase can follow your normal priorities.

### Phase 3: Act on Your Priorities

The act phase is the meat of the session, and the activities you perform depend on your current priorities. For most players, the act phase includes a mix of rolling, building, and raiding, with the exact mix determined by the assess phase.

If rolling is the priority, spend the 50% rolling bucket of your budget on targeted rolls. The targeting can be event-driven (rolling for a featured pet) or collection-driven (rolling to complete a family bonus). The rolling should be done in deliberate batches with breaks in between, not as a continuous stream, because the batch structure lets you stop when your budget is spent without losing track of where you are.

If building is the priority, place the walls, traps, or upgrades that the assess phase identified. The building phase should focus on one improvement at a time, not on building many things at once. A single reinforced wall is more valuable than five cheap walls that do not actually protect anything.

If raiding is the priority, choose a target from the raid interface and execute a raid using the gear and tactics covered in the first raid guide. The raiding phase should include 1 to 3 raids, depending on the available raid energy, and each raid should be evaluated for its profitability and learning value.

### Phase 4: Optimize the Details

The optimize phase is where you make the small improvements that add up to a much stronger base over time. These improvements are not the main activity of the session, but they are the difference between a base that grows and a base that stagnates.

The first optimization is pet leveling. If you have upgrade tokens sitting unused, spend them on the highest-rarity pet you own. Each level increases income by 2.5 percent, and the effect compounds. A pet that you level 10 times during the optimize phase will earn meaningfully more cash over the next month than the same pet left unleveled.

The second optimization is gear maintenance. Check your gear for damaged or expired items, and replace them with fresh ones from the shop. Damaged gear performs worse in raids, and using damaged gear is a common cause of failed raids. The replacement cost is small relative to the cost of a failed raid.

The third optimization is storage hygiene. Check your storage for common pets that are not contributing to family bonuses, and recycle the ones that are pure duplicates. A clean storage is a useful storage, and the discipline of regular cleanup prevents the storage bloat that traps many players.

### Phase 5: Conclude Deliberately

The conclude phase is the most underappreciated part of the daily routine, and the part that beginners most often skip. The conclusion has three steps: position your pets, set up your defenses, and log off at the right moment.

Position your pets inside your strongest defensive layer, not in the outer ring where they are vulnerable. The pets should be in the core vault or in the second layer of walls, depending on your base design. The positioning takes 30 seconds and is the difference between a pet that survives an overnight raid and a pet that gets stolen while you are away.

Set up your defenses to be in their strongest state. This means repairing any damaged walls, replacing any triggered traps, and closing any open entrances. The base should be in a defensive posture, not in the middle of an expansion. Half-finished defenses are weaker than complete defenses, and a half-finished base is more attractive to opportunistic raiders.

Log off at the moment when your base is in its strongest state, your pets are in their safest position, and your offline income will accumulate from the maximum point. The moment you log off starts the offline timer, so the longer you play after your base is fully prepared, the more you delay the start of the offline accumulation. The optimal conclusion is to finish the optimize phase, place your pets, set up your defenses, and then log off within a few minutes.

## Adapting the Routine

The five-phase routine is a template, not a rigid rule. On event days, the act phase shifts toward event-specific activities. On raid-heavy days, the act phase focuses on raids. On update days, the assess phase expands to include reviewing the new content. The template adapts to whatever the current priorities are, while keeping the overall structure that makes the routine effective.

The routine also gets faster with practice. A new player might spend 10 minutes in the claim and assess phases because they do not yet know what to look for. An experienced player can complete the same phases in 3 minutes because they know exactly where to click and what to check. The time saved as the routine becomes automatic can be redirected to the act phase, which is where the actual progression happens.
