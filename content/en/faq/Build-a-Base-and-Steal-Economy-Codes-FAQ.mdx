The economy of Build a Base and Steal is more complex than it looks at first glance, and the code system is one of the most-misunderstood features. ## What is the Main Currency in Build a Base and Steal?

The main currency is cash, which is earned through pet income, raids, codes, and event rewards. Cash is used to buy walls, traps, gear, pet storage, and a few premium items. There is no hard cap on how much cash you can hold, but events, and a few specific raids. Gems are used to buy exclusive pets, premium storage tiers, and the Premium Pass. Gems are rare enough that they are worth saving for the items that can only be purchased with gems, rather than spending them on cash-replaceable items.

### Can I Buy Cash with Robux?

Yes, but the exchange rate is not favorable. The game allows you to convert Robux into cash at roughly 100 cash per Robux, but the same cash can be earned passively through pet income at a rate of several hundred to several thousand cash per minute, depending on your roster. The smart play is to never buy cash with Robux; the conversion rate is so poor that the same Robux is better spent on the Premium Pass or on gem-exclusive items.

The developers intentionally make the Robux-to-cash conversion bad, because the game's economy is designed to reward active and passive play, not direct purchases. Players who try to buy their way to the top with Robux find that they are quickly out-earned by players who invested the same amount of time in rolling and base building.

## How Do Codes Work?

Codes in Build a Base and Steal are short text strings that grant free rewards when redeemed through the official Discord, the official X account, YouTube creator partnerships, and update launch events.

Each code has a limited number of total redemptions across the entire player base, not just per account. The most popular codes (like launch codes) can be claimed by 50,000 to 100,000 players before they run out. Less popular codes may go unclaimed for days, especially if they are tied to a specific creator's audience. The fastest way to claim codes is to redeem them within the first few hours of release.

## How Can I Make Cash Faster?

The fastest ways to make cash are: maximize your pet roster, complete as many families as possible, level up your best pets, and play during server events. The compound effect of these four actions is far more impactful than any one of them alone, and most top players have spent months optimizing all four.

The second-fastest way is to raid, but raiding is a time investment rather than a passive income source. A successful raid can produce 30,000 to 100,000 cash worth of pets, plus the gear you saved by not buying them directly. Raiding is most efficient when you have a clear target in mind and a full set of gear, and least efficient when you are wandering the server hoping to find something.

A balanced approach that funds rolling, defense, and storage together is the path to long-term growth.
