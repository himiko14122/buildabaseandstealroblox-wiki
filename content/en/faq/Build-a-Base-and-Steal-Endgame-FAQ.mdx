Endgame players in Build a Base and Steal face different challenges than beginners. The questions shift from how to get started to how to optimize every aspect of the game for maximum efficiency. This FAQ compiles the most frequently asked questions from advanced players and provides detailed answers about mythic gear, rebirth strategies, and endgame progression.

## Mythic Gear

### **Q:** What is the fastest way to obtain mythic gear?


**A:** The fastest way to obtain mythic gear is a combination of rolling during events, grinding tier 5 boss encounters, and crafting. Events multiply drop rates by 2-3x, so save your raid attempts for event windows. Tier 5 bosses have a 3-5% drop rate for mythic gear, and they also drop crafting materials. Crafting is the guaranteed path, but it requires rare materials from tier 5 bosses. The optimal strategy is to roll during events, grind tier 5 bosses, and craft the mythic items that have the lowest drop rates.

### **Q:** Which mythic gear should I craft first?


**A:** Craft the mythic items that have the lowest drop rates and the highest utility. The mythic warhammer has a 1% drop rate but is the strongest weapon, so craft it first. The mythic rope has a 2% drop rate and is essential for traversal, so craft it second. The mythic pickaxe has a 1% drop rate and is the best general-purpose tool, so craft it third. Avoid crafting items with drop rates above 2%, because rolling for those is more efficient than crafting.

### **Q:** How do I obtain mythic stars for crafting?


**A:** Mythic stars drop only from tier 5 boss encounters at a rate of roughly 1%. Tier 5 bosses are the hardest content in the game, and they require a full set of legendary gear to defeat. The grind for mythic stars is the bottleneck in the crafting system, and it may take dozens of tier 5 boss defeats to collect enough stars for a single mythic craft. The optimal strategy is to focus exclusively on tier 5 bosses once you have legendary gear, and to accept that the grind will take time.

## Rebirth and Prestige

### **Q:** When should I do my first rebirth?


**A:** Do your first rebirth when you have reached account level 30, when you have a pet roster of 20-25 pets with at least 5 legendaries, and when you have exhausted the progression options in your current server. Rebirthing too early wipes out weeks of progress for a small multiplier gain. Rebirthing too late wastes time that could be spent building prestige. The sweet spot is when your progression has plateaued but you still have a strong enough roster to rebuild quickly.

### **Q:** How do I maximize the prestige multiplier gain per rebirth?


**A:** Meet as many bonus conditions as possible. The conditions include: reaching 25 pets before rebirth, holding at least one mythic at rebirth, completing the run without any losses, and rebirthing during an event. Each condition adds roughly 0.02-0.03x to the multiplier gain. Meeting all four conditions can add 0.08-0.12x compared to meeting none, which compounds significantly over multiple rebirths.

### **Q:** What is the optimal rebirth cycle length?


**A:** The optimal rebirth cycle length is when you have plateaued in your current cycle, typically after 2-4 weeks of active play. Plateauing is indicated by flat daily income despite active play, and by exhaustion of progression options. The cycle length varies by player skill and investment, but 2-4 weeks is typical for dedicated players. Shorter cycles sacrifice multiplier gain, while longer cycles waste time that could be spent building prestige.

## Offline Income Optimization

### **Q:** How do I maximize my offline income?


**A:** Maximize offline income by optimizing four factors: pet rarity, happiness, aura placement, and login frequency. Fill your roster with the highest-rarity pets you can obtain, keep happiness above 150% through feeding and rotation, place three auras in a triangle pattern around your highest-value pets, and log in twice per day to reset the 24-hour cap. The combination of these four factors can increase offline income by 5-10x compared to an unoptimized setup.

### **Q:** Should I buy the premium 72-hour cap extension?


**A:** The premium extension is worth it if you cannot log in at least once every 24 hours. The extension lets you collect three days of accumulation in a single session, which is valuable for players with unpredictable schedules. For players who can log in twice daily, the premium extension is less compelling, because the natural 24-hour cap is already larger than their typical absence. The break-even point is roughly 30 days of premium access for an average player.

### **Q:** How does the prestige multiplier interact with offline income?


**A:** The prestige multiplier applies to offline income exactly as it applies to active income. A 1.5x prestige multiplier generates 50% more offline income with identical pets and base configuration. The multiplier also affects the cap calculation, so a 1.5x multiplier causes you to hit the cap in 16 hours instead of 24. This means high-prestige players should log in more frequently to avoid wasting accumulation time.

## Advanced Strategies

### **Q:** What is the optimal strategy for the current meta?


**A:** The current meta favors defensive bases with multi-layered walls and dense trap clusters. Offensive raids should focus on speedrunning with mythic gear, using the Mythic Warhammer for splash damage and the Mythic Rope for bypassing walls. Pet rolling should focus on events, with the Eternal Phoenix and Quantum Leviathan being the top targets due to their strong special abilities. The meta shifts periodically, so stay updated with the latest community analysis.

### **Q:** How do I dominate a server as an alpha raider?


**A:** Dominate a server by raiding constantly, targeting the weakest bases first, and building a reputation that discourages other players from raiding your base. Raid every base in the server at least once to establish dominance. Then focus on farming the weakest bases repeatedly, because the profit-per-hour is highest against weak bases. Defend your base aggressively by raiding back any player who raids you. Consistency and ruthlessness are more important than having the strongest gear.

### **Q:** Should I play solo or join a team?


**A:** Both playstyles are viable for endgame players. Solo play offers maximum flexibility and control, but requires self-reliance in every aspect. Team play offers shared resources and coordinated raids, but requires coordination. The choice depends on your personality and schedule. Many endgame players use a hybrid approach, playing solo most of the time but joining teams for specific events or goals.

## Technical and Mathematical Questions

### **Q:** What is the exact formula for offline income?


**A:** The simplified formula is `Offline Income = Total Pet Rate × Absence Seconds × Event Bonus × Aura Coverage × Server Mood × (1 - Diminishing Returns Penalty)`. The diminishing returns penalty depends on pet count, roughly 0.7 for 20 pets, 0.55 for 30 pets, and 0.4 for 40 pets. The cap is calculated based on effective pet production time, not real time, so event bonuses cause you to hit the cap faster.

### **Q:** How do the set bonuses interact with the prestige multiplier?


**A:** Set bonuses and prestige multiplier stack multiplicatively. A 5-piece Fire Set bonus provides +20% damage, and a 1.5x prestige multiplier provides +50% income. The combined effect is 1.2 × 1.5 = 1.8x, which is an 80% total increase. The stacking is multiplicative, not additive, so combining multiple bonuses produces exponential gains.

### **Q:** What is the mathematical optimal pet roster size?


**A:** The optimal pet roster size is 20-25 pets. Below 20, you are not fully utilizing the diminishing returns curve. Above 25, the per-pet effectiveness drops sharply due to diminishing returns, and the average income per pet decreases. The exact optimal size depends on your server's pet prices and your ability to refresh legendaries, but the 20-25 range is optimal for most players.

## Future Planning

### **Q:** What should I focus on after reaching the prestige plateau?


**A:** After reaching the prestige plateau, focus on refining your base configuration and optimizing your income. Transition from progression-focused play to optimization-focused play. Experiment with different aura placements, test different pet combinations, and refine your raiding technique. The plateau is the end of the progression journey and the beginning of the optimization journey.

### **Q:** How do I prepare for major updates?


**A:** Prepare for major updates by stocking up on cash, materials, and rolling currency. Major updates often introduce new pets, new gear, or new mechanics that require significant investment. Having a stockpile allows you to adopt new content immediately rather than waiting to grind resources. Also, stay updated with developer announcements to understand what changes are coming.

Endgame progression is about optimization rather than expansion. Every percentage point of efficiency matters, and the players who master the details are the ones who reach the top of the leaderboards.
