New players to Build a Base and Steal have many questions, from how to get started to how to avoid common traps. This FAQ compiles the most frequently asked questions from beginners and provides clear, actionable answers that will help you progress efficiently and avoid costly mistakes.

## Getting Started

### **Q:** What should I do first when I join Build a Base and Steal?


**A:** The first thing you should do is place a single wall to mark where your base entrance will be. Then, take one roll to understand the pet mechanics. After that, roll 3-5 more times and place those pets inside the area you intend to protect. Build a simple perimeter with 3-4 walls and one controlled entrance. This gives you a functioning base with a small pet roster and a clear next step for your second session.

### **Q:** How much cash should I spend on rolling in my first session?


**A:** Spend roughly 50% of your starting cash on rolling, 30% on walls and defenses, and hold back 20% as a reserve. This 50/30/20 split ensures that you have pets to generate income, defenses to protect those pets, and a reserve for emergencies. Spending more than 50% on rolling in the first session is the most common beginner mistake.

### **Q:** When should I start raiding other bases?


**A:** Start raiding only after you have completed your first layer of walls and have at least 5 pets in your roster. Your first raid should target a base that is clearly weaker than yours, with fewer walls and lower-value pets. A successful first raid builds confidence and provides a small starting nest egg. Attempting difficult raids too early is a recipe for frustration.

## Core Mechanics

### **Q:** How does offline income work?


**A:** Offline income is calculated based on your pet collection and the duration of your absence. Each pet generates income based on its rarity, happiness, and aura bonuses. The accumulation is capped at 24 hours of effective production time. When you log back in, you collect the accumulated income. The cap resets when you collect, so logging in twice per day produces more income than logging in once per day.

### **Q:** What is the difference between pet rarities?


**A:** Pets come in six rarities: common, uncommon, rare, epic, legendary, and mythic. Higher rarity pets generate more income per second, have higher stat caps, and often have stronger special abilities. The income difference between rarities is substantial: a mythic pet generates roughly 10-20x the income of a common pet. However, higher rarity pets are also much rarer and more expensive to obtain.

### **Q:** How do walls and traps work together?


**A:** Walls slow down raiders and protect your pets, while traps damage raiders who get too close. Walls are the primary defense, and traps are the secondary defense. The optimal base design uses walls to funnel raiders into trap kill zones, where multiple traps can attack the raider simultaneously. Walls alone are insufficient against determined raiders, but traps alone are insufficient against raiders with strong gear.

## Common Mistakes

### **Q:** What is the most common mistake new players make?


**A:** The most common mistake is spending all starting cash on rolling and building no defenses. New players often roll ten times, get five commons, and then realize they cannot afford any walls. They log off with an unraided pile of low-tier pets that generate almost no income and could be stolen at any moment. The fix is to always spend at least 30% of your cash on defenses in your first session.

### **Q:** Why do my pets generate so little income?


**A:** Low income is typically caused by three factors: low pet rarity, low happiness, and missing aura bonuses. Commons generate minimal income, and even legendaries generate significantly less if their happiness is low. Placing auras near your pets can increase their income by 25-40%. Address all three factors to maximize your pet income.

### **Q:** I keep getting raided and losing my pets. What should I do?


**A:** If you are constantly raided, your defenses are insufficient. Add more walls, especially around your most valuable pets. Place traps in dense clusters near the entrance to create kill zones. Consider moving to a different server with fewer aggressive raiders. The goal is to make your base too expensive to raid, so raiders will target easier bases instead.

## Progression

### **Q:** How do I progress faster?


**A:** The fastest progression comes from three activities: rolling for high-rarity pets during events, upgrading your gear to epic and legendary, and raiding efficiently. Events multiply drop rates, so save your rolling budget for events. Better gear makes raids faster, which allows you to obtain more pets in less time. Combine all three for maximum progression.

### **Q:** When should I upgrade my gear?


**A:** Upgrade your gear as soon as you can afford it, but do not upgrade unevenly. Upgrade your pickaxe to uncommon, then your hammer to uncommon, then your rope to uncommon, and then start upgrading to rare. Keep your entire loadout at roughly the same rarity level, because having one weak item creates a bottleneck.

### **Q:** What is the rebirth system and when should I use it?


**A:** Rebirthing resets your base, cash, and pet roster, but grants a permanent prestige multiplier that applies to all future income. You should rebirth when you have reached account level 30, when you have 20-25 pets with at least 5 legendaries, and when you have exhausted the progression options in your current server. Rebirthing too early will slow your progression rather than accelerate it.

## Technical Issues

### **Q:** My offline income seems lower than expected. What is wrong?


**A:** Low offline income is typically caused by hitting the cap. If you were away for more than 24 hours, your pets stopped accumulating after 24 hours of effective production time. The cap is also reached faster during events, because the event bonus increases production. Log in more frequently to avoid hitting the cap.

### **Q:** The game feels slow. How can I speed it up?


**A:** The game feels slow when you are stuck in a progression plateau. Break through the plateau by upgrading your gear, by rolling during events, or by rebirthing. A single gear upgrade or a successful event can dramatically speed up the game. If you have exhausted all progression options, rebirthing is the only way to reset the plateau.

### **Q:** I keep failing raids. What am I doing wrong?


**A:** Failed raids are typically caused by attempting bases that are too difficult for your current gear level, or by poor route planning. Start with easier bases and gradually work up to harder ones. Analyze the base layout before entering, and identify the critical path to the valuable pets. Avoid trap clusters if possible, or clear them with area-of-effect damage.

## Community and Social

### **Q:** Should I join a team or play solo?


**A:** Both playstyles are viable. Solo play offers maximum flexibility and control, but requires self-reliance. Team play offers shared resources and coordinated raids, but requires coordination. Choose based on your personality and schedule. Many players use a hybrid approach, playing solo most of the time but joining teams for events.

### **Q:** How do I find a good server?


**A:** A good server has a mix of player levels, active trading, and reasonable raiding activity. Avoid servers that are dominated by a few alpha raiders, as these servers are difficult for new players. Look for servers with an active chat and a helpful community. Server hopping is common, and you can try several servers before finding one that fits your playstyle.

### **Q:** What are codes and how do I use them?


**A:** Codes are promotional strings that you can redeem for free rewards, such as cash, pets, or gear. Codes are released by the developers on social media and during events. To redeem a code, enter it in the codes section of the settings menu. Codes are time-limited, so redeem them as soon as possible.
