New players to Build a Base and Steal have a lot of questions, and the in-game tutorial only covers about a third of what you actually need to know to play well. This FAQ collects the most common general questions and gives short, actionable answers. If you are brand new to the game, reading this entire page before your first session will save you hours of trial and error.

## What is Build a Base and Steal?

Build a Base and Steal is a Roblox tycoon and base defense hybrid where you build a private base, populate it with pets you earn by rolling, defend those pets from raids by other players, and then raid other players' bases to steal their pets. The game blends single-player base building with multiplayer raiding, and it has an offline money system that lets your pets keep generating cash while you are away.

The game is free-to-play on Roblox, with optional Robux purchases for cosmetic upgrades, storage expansions, and the Premium Pass. Most of the game's content is accessible without spending any money, but the Premium Pass significantly accelerates progression and is worth considering for serious players.

### Is Build a Base and Steal Cross-Platform?

Yes. Build a Base and Steal runs on every platform that supports modern Roblox, including PC, Mac, iOS, Android, Xbox, and PlayStation. Your account and progress are tied to your Roblox account, so you can switch devices freely and pick up where you left off. The only platform-specific limitation is that touch screens have a slightly less precise building interface, but the game is fully playable on mobile.

Cross-platform play also means that you can be raided by players on any device. Mobile players can raid PC players and vice versa, which is part of what makes the multiplayer economy interesting. Don't assume that a base designed on PC will look the same to a mobile raider; the touch interface gives raiders slightly different building tools than the mouse interface gives defenders.

## What is the Core Gameplay Loop?

The core loop has four parts: roll for pets, place pets in your base, build defenses to protect them, and raid other players' bases for more pets. The four parts feed into each other in a cycle that the game describes as "Build, Defend, Raid, Grow." You start by rolling pets with starter cash, then place your best pets in a defended base, then use your defenses and your pet collection to fuel raids on other players, and the rewards from raids fund more rolling and more building.

The loop is intentionally endless. There is no "win condition" or final boss in Build a Base and Steal. The goal is to keep growing your base, your pet collection, and your offline income until you are one of the top players on the leaderboard. The leaderboard resets every month, which gives the game a long-term competitive structure.

### How Long is a "Session"?

A typical Build a Base and Steal session for a new player is 30 to 60 minutes. In that window, you can roll a starting pet collection, build a basic base, claim daily rewards, and complete a few raids. The game is designed to be friendly to short play sessions, because the offline money system means you do not need to grind for hours to keep up.

Experienced players tend to have longer sessions, often 2 to 4 hours, because they have more complex bases to manage and more active raids to coordinate. The longest sessions are usually during update launches and limited-time events, when there is new content to explore and limited-time rewards to chase.

## Can I Play Solo or With Friends?

Both. The game is fully playable solo, and most of the base building and rolling content is single-player. The raid system is where multiplayer comes in: you can raid other players in your public server, or you can join a private server with friends and raid each other in a controlled environment. Private servers are popular with friends who want to test base designs without competing against strangers.

There is also a co-op base defense mode that becomes available once you have built at least 10 walls. In co-op mode, you and a friend can share a base and split the defense responsibilities. The rewards are shared, but the income is also shared, so co-op is most valuable for players who enjoy the social aspect more than the optimization aspect.

### What is the Age Rating?

Build a Base and Steal is rated 9+ on Roblox. There is no violence, no gore, and no mature content. The game does involve stealing pets from other players, but the in-game presentation is cartoony and family-friendly. The Roblox platform itself has stricter controls for under-13 accounts, and some features like trading may be restricted for those accounts, but the core gameplay is accessible to all ages.

## Is My Progress Saved?

Yes. Your progress is automatically saved to Roblox's cloud servers every few minutes, and any disconnect or crash will lose at most a few minutes of progress. Your offline money is calculated server-side, so you do not need to be online at the moment the timer expires to claim your cash. The only scenarios where progress can be lost are extended internet outages, account-level violations, and rare server bugs that the developers patch quickly.

If you ever notice that your progress is missing or your offline cash is incorrect, the first step is to relog and check again. The second step is to submit a support ticket through the in-game menu. The third step is to check the official Discord for known issues, because the developers post actively when bugs affect save data.

### What Happens If I Get Banned?

Roblox-level bans are extremely rare for legitimate Build a Base and Steal players. The game's anti-cheat is focused on detecting exploit tools and AFK scripts, not on punishing borderline play. If you are banned, the most common cause is using a third-party tool that injects code into the Roblox client, which violates Roblox's terms of service across all games. Appeals are handled through Roblox's support system, not through the Build a Base and Steal developers.

For temporary issues, the game has its own punishment system that ranges from warnings to temporary raid bans. The most common trigger is repeated friendly-fire damage in co-op mode, which the game interprets as griefing. If you are hit with a temporary ban, the duration is usually 24 hours and the affected feature is raids only.

### Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

New players in the faq area of Build a Base and Steal tend to make a predictable set of mistakes, and most of these mistakes are not obvious until you have made them a few times. This section lists the most common pitfalls and explains how to avoid each one.

**Pitfall one: over-investing in a single base design.** The temptation is to base that worked in the early game will not work in the mid game. The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Plan for base evolution from the start, and design walls and traps that can be upgraded incrementally without rebuilding from scratch.

The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Set up your base for maximum offline accumulation, position your best pets in the core vault, and log off deliberately when your base is in its strongest state.

The result is that the intended goal is not achieved. The fix is to Build a collection of A and B tier pets that provide reliable income and good family bonuses, then use the income to roll for S tier pets when events boost their drop rates.

The fix is to Focus on one area for an entire session, then switch. Spreading is for established players who can afford to do everything at once; focus is for progression.
