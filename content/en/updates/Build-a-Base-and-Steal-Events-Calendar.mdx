## Event Types Explained

### Pet Rolling Events
Pet rolling events increase the drop rate for specific pet types or specific pets. These events are the best time to roll for pets, because the increased drop rate dramatically improves your chances of obtaining high-rarity pets.

**Strategy**: Save your rolling budget for the event, roll in large batches to maximize the pity system, and focus on the featured pets first.

### Gear Events
Gear events increase the drop rate for specific gear sets or gear types. These events are the best time to obtain gear, because the increased drop rate makes it easier to complete gear sets.

**Strategy**: Raid heavily during gear events, focus on the featured gear set, and use the increased drop rate to complete missing pieces of the set.

### General Events
General events provide bonuses to multiple aspects of the game, such as offline income, raid rewards, or trading. These events are versatile and benefit all playstyles.

**Strategy**: Adjust your playstyle to maximize the specific bonus. For offline income events, log in frequently. For raid reward events, raid constantly. For trading events, focus on buying and selling.

### Competitive Events
Competitive events are focused on PvP competition, such as faction wars or tournaments. These events have leaderboards and exclusive rewards for top performers.

**Strategy**: Commit fully to the competition, optimize your build for the specific competition format, and coordinate with teammates if applicable.
