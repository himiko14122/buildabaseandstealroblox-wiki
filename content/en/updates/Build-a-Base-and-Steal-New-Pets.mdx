## New Pets in Version 3.5.0

### Celestial Phoenix (Mythic)
The Celestial Phoenix is the newest addition to the mythic tier and immediately became a top-tier meta pet. It has a base rate of 950 per second and provides a 15% aura bonus to all celestial-type pets. Its special ability, "Solar Flare," randomly doubles the income of all pets in the base for 60 seconds, with a trigger chance of 5% every 5 minutes.

**Drop Rate**: 0.01% in standard rolls, 0.05% during Phoenix-themed events
**Meta Impact**: S-tier offensive pet that competes directly with the Eternal Phoenix. The global income doubling effect makes it valuable for any build, not just celestial-themed bases.

### Void Dragon (Mythic)
The Void Dragon is a defensive mythic pet with strong utility. It has a base rate of 900 per second and provides a 10% aura bonus to all dark-type pets. Its special ability, "Void Shield," makes adjacent pets immune to theft for 90 seconds when a raider enters the base.

**Drop Rate**: 0.012% in standard rolls, 0.06% during Dragon-themed events
**Meta Impact**: A-tier defensive pet that is essential for PvP servers. The theft immunity effect is more reliable than the Cosmic Dragon's conditional ability, making it the preferred defensive option for serious players.

### Star Fox (Legendary)
The Star Fox is a utility-focused legendary pet with unique traversal abilities. It has a base rate of 650 per second and provides a 8% aura bonus to all celestial-type pets. Its special ability, "Stardust Dash," increases the player's movement speed by 50% for 30 seconds, with a trigger chance of 15% every 3 minutes.

**Drop Rate**: 0.15% in standard rolls, 0.5% during Fox-themed events
**Meta Impact**: B-tier utility pet that is valuable for speedrunners and traversal-focused players. The movement speed bonus synergizes well with the Shadow Set bonuses.

### Nebula Wolf (Epic)
The Nebula Wolf is an early-mid game epic pet with decent stats and useful utility. It has a base rate of 400 per second and provides a 5% aura bonus to all dark-type pets. Its special ability, "Cosmic Howl," increases the effectiveness of all auras in the base by 10% for 60 seconds, with a trigger chance of 10% every 5 minutes.

**Drop Rate**: 0.5% in standard rolls, 1.5% during Wolf-themed events
**Meta Impact**: B-tier utility pet that is valuable for base optimization. The aura effectiveness bonus is subtle but compounds with multiple auras, making it useful for focused base builders.

## New Pets in Version 3.4.0

### Shadow Stalker (Mythic)
The Shadow Stalker is a stealth-focused mythic pet that was introduced with the Shadow Gear Set. It has a base rate of 880 per second and provides a 12% aura bonus to all shadow-type pets. Its special ability, "Phantom Step," has a 10% chance to make the player invisible for 30 seconds during raids, allowing them to bypass traps undetected.

**Drop Rate**: 0.011% in standard rolls, 0.055% during Shadow-themed events
**Meta Impact**: A-tier utility pet that revolutionized raiding strategies. The invisibility effect makes difficult raids significantly easier, especially in trap-heavy bases.

### Midnight Cat (Legendary)
The Midnight Cat is a charming legendary pet with strong utility. It has a base rate of 620 per second and provides a 7% aura bonus to all shadow-type pets. Its special ability, "Lucky Paws," has a 5% chance to double the cash reward from a successful raid.

**Drop Rate**: 0.14% in standard rolls, 0.45% during Cat-themed events
**Meta Impact**: B-tier utility pet that is valuable for profit-focused raiders. The cash doubling effect is random but can provide substantial rewards over time.

### Ghost Hound (Epic)
The Ghost Hound is a spooky epic pet with defensive utility. It has a base rate of 380 per second and provides a 4% aura bonus to all shadow-type pets. Its special ability, "Spectral Bark," has a 15% chance to scare away raiders, causing them to flee the base immediately.

**Drop Rate**: 0.45% in standard rolls, 1.4% during Hound-themed events
**Meta Impact**: C-tier defensive pet that is situational but powerful in the right context. The scare effect is random but can save valuable pets from theft.
