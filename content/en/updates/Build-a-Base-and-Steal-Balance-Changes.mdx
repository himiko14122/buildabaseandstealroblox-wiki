## Major Balance Changes in Version 3.5.0

### Pet Drop Rate Adjustments
**Legendary Drop Rate**: Increased from 2% to 2.5%
**Impact**: This change makes legendary pets significantly more accessible, especially for new and mid-game players. The increased drop rate reduces the time required to build a competitive roster, and it makes legendary pets more viable as stepping stones to mythic pets. The change also increases the supply of legendary pets on the trading market, lowering prices.

**Mythic Drop Rate**: Decreased from 1% to 0.8%
**Impact**: This change makes mythic pets more exclusive and harder to obtain. The decreased drop rate increases the value of existing mythic pets and makes mythic pets more of a long-term goal. The change also encourages players to focus on crafting mythic gear rather than relying solely on rolling.

### Gear Durability Overhaul
**Mythic Gear Durability**: Reduced durability cost by 20%
**Impact**: This change makes mythic gear more sustainable for long raiding sessions. Previously, mythic gear would break after 5-6 hours of continuous raiding, which limited its usefulness for marathon sessions. The reduced durability cost allows mythic gear to last 7-8 hours, making it more viable for extended play sessions.

**Epic Weapon Damage**: Increased base damage by 10%
**Impact**: This change bridges the gap between epic and legendary gear. Epic weapons are now roughly 30% weaker than legendary weapons, compared to 40% before the change. This makes epic gear more viable for mid-game players who cannot yet afford legendary gear.

### Raid Difficulty Scaling
**Trap Damage Scaling**: Increased by 15%
**Impact**: This change makes late-game raids significantly more challenging, especially for bases with dense trap clusters. The increased damage scaling forces raiders to invest more in healing and to be more careful about route planning. Defensive bases with heavy trap investments become more rewarding to build and more difficult to raid.
