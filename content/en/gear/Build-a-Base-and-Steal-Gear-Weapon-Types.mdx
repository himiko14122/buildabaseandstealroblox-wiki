## Blasters: The All-Purpose Weapon

The blaster is the default weapon in Build a Base and Steal, and it is the most versatile of all the weapon types. Blasters deal consistent damage at medium range, they can be used to break walls, and they are effective against defenders who are caught in the open. A blaster is not the best at any single task, but it is good enough at all of them, which makes it the most popular weapon among beginners and intermediate players.

The blaster comes in three rarity tiers: basic, advanced, and legendary. The basic blaster is the starter weapon and is recommended for new players. The advanced blaster provides a 50 percent damage boost and is the typical mid-game weapon. The legendary blaster provides a 3x damage boost over the basic version and is the end-game goal for blaster users.

The blaster is best for players who enjoy a flexible playstyle and who do not want to specialize in any single aspect of raiding. It is also the best weapon for solo raiders, because its versatility allows it to adapt to whatever the target base throws at it. A hammer can destroy a basic wall in two hits, while a blaster might take five or six. The trade-off is that hammers are slow, they have limited range, and they are not effective against defenders.

The hammer is best for players who specialize in fast breach raids, where the goal is to break into the base, grab the loot, and extract as quickly as possible. Hammer users can clear a base in seconds if they know the layout, and they are the bane of players who build elaborate wall systems without adequate defenses.

The hammer is weakest against base designs that include multiple layers of walls, traps, and chokepoints. A hammer user who encounters a layered defense will spend most of their time breaking walls rather than fighting defenders, and they will be vulnerable to counter-attacks. Players who want to use a hammer effectively should target bases with simple layouts and weak walls.

### Energy Weapons: The Anti-Defender Specialist

Energy weapons in Build a Base and Steal are the anti-defender specialists, and they deal high damage to enemy players while dealing less damage to walls. An energy weapon user is at effective against walls, and they require careful aim to maximize their damage potential.

Energy weapon users are also effective in counter-raids, where they need to defend their own base from attackers.

Energy weapons are weakest against bases that are heavily walled and lightly defended. A player with an energy weapon who tries to break through a heavily walled base will spend most of their time on walls, which is the weakness of the energy weapon class. ### Sniper Weapons: The Long-Range Specialist

Sniper weapons are the long-range specialists, and they deal massive damage at long range while dealing almost no damage at close range. A sniper weapon user can eliminate defenders and destroy walls from a safe distance, but they are nearly helpless if an attacker gets close. Sniper weapons are the most skill-intensive weapons in the game, and they require excellent aim and positioning to use effectively.

Sniper weapons are best for experienced players who have mastered the positioning game and who can pick off defenders from outside the base. A skilled sniper can take out an entire defender team without taking any damage, and they are the most feared counter-attackers in the game. Sniper weapons are also effective for taking out high-value pets from outside the base, without have long internal corridors with limited sight lines. A sniper user who is caught in a corridor with an enemy at close range will lose the fight 9 times out of 10, and the best counter to a sniper is to push them aggressively.

### Support Weapons: The Utility Specialist

The support weapon class is a unique category that includes weapons that provide utility benefits rather than raw damage. Examples include healing guns that heal allies, scan guns that reveal enemy positions, and slow guns that slow enemy movement. Support weapons are not used to deal damage directly, but they enable the rest of the team to deal more damage and survive longer.

Support weapons are best for team play, where one player can focus on support while the others focus on damage. A well-coordinated team with a support player will outperform a team of equal skill that lacks support, because the support player enables the team to take more risks and recover from mistakes.

Support weapons are weakest in solo play, where the support player has no one to support. Solo support players are usually ineffective, because they cannot deal enough damage to complete a raid on their own. Support weapons are a niche choice that is only viable in team play, but in the right team, they are invaluable.
