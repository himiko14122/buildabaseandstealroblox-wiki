Epic tier gear is the backbone of mid-game progression in Build a Base and Steal. It is stronger than rare gear but more accessible than legendary gear, and it serves as the bridge between the early game and the late game. Epic gear is not just about stronger stats; it is about the utility it provides, from specialized tools that make raiding faster to defensive items that make your base harder to breach. This guide ranks the best epic gear, explains how to obtain it, and shows you how to build an optimized epic loadout.

## How Epic Gear Differs from Lower Tiers

Epic gear in Build a Base and Steal has three main advantages over rare and uncommon gear. First, epic weapons have higher damage values, which means they breach walls and destroy traps faster. Second, epic tools have higher durability, which means they last longer during long raiding sessions. Third, epic gear often has special effects or abilities that rare gear lacks, such as splash damage on hammers or range bonuses on pickaxes.

The difference in effectiveness is significant. An epic pickaxe breaks walls roughly 30% faster than a rare pickaxe of the same level. An epic hammer destroys traps roughly 50% faster than a rare hammer. An epic rope has 2x the durability of a rare rope, which means it lasts twice as long during multi-floor raids. For players who are transitioning from the early game to the mid game, upgrading to epic gear is the single most impactful gear upgrade they can make.

### Where Epic Gear Drops

Epic gear in Build a Base and Steal drops from three main sources: high-level raids, boss encounters, and the gear shop. High-level raids are the most reliable source, because the drop rate for epic gear is roughly 10% from raids on bases with a gear score above 500. Boss encounters have a 5-10% drop rate for epic gear, but bosses spawn infrequently, so the total volume of epic drops from bosses is lower than from raids.

The gear shop is the third source, and it sells a rotating selection of epic gear each day. The prices are high, but the gear is guaranteed, which makes the shop a good option for players who need a specific epic item and are willing to pay a premium. The optimal strategy is to check the gear shop every day and buy any epic items that fill gaps in your loadout, rather than relying solely on raids to provide the specific items you need.

## Epic Tier Weapons

### Epic Sledgehammer
The Epic Sledgehammer is the best offensive weapon in the epic tier. It deals 500 damage per hit to walls, which is the highest in the epic tier, and it has a special effect that deals splash damage to adjacent walls. The splash damage is 25% of the base damage, so the hammer effectively deals 500 damage to the target wall and 125 damage to each adjacent wall. The splash effect makes the hammer extremely efficient against multi-wall bases.

The Epic Sledgehammer drops from high-level raids at a rate of roughly 5%. It also appears in the gear shop occasionally, priced at 5,000 cash. The hammer is a must-have for any mid-game player who plans to raid seriously. The splash damage alone is worth the upgrade from rare gear, and the high base damage makes the hammer the fastest wall-breaker in the epic tier.

### Epic Dynamite
The Epic Dynamite is a specialized explosive that deals 1,000 damage to all walls and traps within a 3-tile radius. The damage is area-of-effect, which means dynamite is ideal for clearing clustered defenses. The dynamite also has a secondary effect that stuns all raiders in the blast radius for 2 seconds, which gives you a brief window to follow up with a second attack.

The Epic Dynamite drops from boss encounters at a rate of roughly 8%. It also appears in the gear shop rarely, priced at 10,000 cash. The dynamite is situational but powerful. Against bases with clustered defenses, dynamite can clear an entire layer of walls and traps in a single use. Against bases with spread-out defenses, dynamite is less effective, and a hammer or pickaxe would be a better choice.

### Epic Crossbow
The Epic Crossbow is a ranged weapon that deals 300 damage per shot and has a range of 10 tiles. The crossbow fires projectiles that penetrate walls, so you can shoot through walls to hit traps or other targets on the other side. The crossbow also has a secondary effect that increases the damage of your next melee attack by 50% if the crossbow shot hits a target.

The Epic Crossbow drops from raids on bases with high trap counts at a rate of roughly 6%. It also appears in the gear shop occasionally, priced at 6,000 cash. The crossbow is a strong choice for players who prefer ranged raiding over melee raiding. The penetration effect is valuable for bases with layered defenses, and the melee damage synergy makes the crossbow a versatile addition to any loadout.

## Epic Tier Tools

### Epic Pickaxe
The Epic Pickaxe is the best general-purpose tool in the epic tier. It deals 400 damage per hit to walls and 200 damage per hit to traps, and it has a special effect that increases the damage of successive hits by 10% for each hit on the same wall. The effect caps at 50%, so a five-hit combo deals 400, 440, 480, 520, and 560 damage.

The Epic Pickaxe drops from raids on bases with high wall counts at a rate of roughly 7%. It also appears in the gear shop frequently, priced at 4,000 cash. The pickaxe is the most reliable tool for general raiding, because it works well against both walls and traps, and the combo damage makes it faster than a hammer against single walls. The pickaxe is the first epic tool any mid-game player should obtain.

### Epic Rope
The Epic Rope is a traversal tool that lets you climb between floors. The epic rope has 2x the durability of the rare rope, which means it can be used twice as many times before breaking. The rope also has a special effect that reduces the fall damage you take by 50%, which makes it valuable for multi-floor bases with long drops.

The Epic Rope drops from raids on multi-floor bases at a rate of roughly 8%. It also appears in the gear shop occasionally, priced at 3,000 cash. The rope is essential for any player who raids multi-floor bases, because the increased durability means you can complete longer raids without the rope breaking mid-climb. The fall damage reduction is a nice bonus that makes the rope safer to use in risky situations.

### Epic Medkit
The Epic Medkit is a healing item that restores 200 health over 10 seconds. The epic medkit has a secondary effect that increases the effectiveness of all healing items used in the next 30 seconds by 25%, which makes it valuable for chaining healing items during tough raids. The medkit also has a tertiary effect that removes all debuffs, such as trap stuns or poison.

The Epic Medkit drops from boss encounters at a rate of roughly 10%. It also appears in the gear shop rarely, priced at 2,500 cash. The medkit is essential for any player who raids high-level bases, because the healing and debuff removal can turn a near-death situation into a successful raid. The chaining effect is powerful when combined with other healing items, such as potions or bandages.

## Building an Epic Loadout

### The General-Purpose Loadout
The optimal general-purpose epic loadout consists of the Epic Pickaxe as the primary tool, the Epic Sledgehammer as the secondary weapon, the Epic Rope for traversal, and the Epic Medkit for healing. This loadout covers all the essential raiding functions: breaking walls, destroying traps, climbing floors, and healing damage. The loadout is balanced and works well against most base configurations.

The general-purpose loadout is the best starting point for mid-game players. It is not specialized for any specific base type, but it is flexible enough to handle most situations. As players gain experience and identify which base types they raid most frequently, they can customize the loadout to better suit their playstyle.

### The Speed-Run Loadout
The speed-run loadout is optimized for fast raids against single-floor bases. It consists of the Epic Sledgehammer as the primary weapon, the Epic Dynamite for clearing clustered defenses, and the Epic Medkit for emergency healing. The loadout excludes traversal tools like the rope, because it assumes you are raiding single-floor bases where traversal is not needed.

The speed-run loadout is the best choice for players who farm specific base types repeatedly and want to maximize their raids-per-hour. The high damage of the hammer and dynamite makes the loadout extremely fast against bases with clustered defenses, and the medkit provides a safety net for mistakes.

### The Multi-Floor Loadout
The multi-floor loadout is optimized for raids against multi-floor bases with long vertical climbs. It consists of the Epic Pickaxe as the primary tool, the Epic Rope for traversal, and two Epic Medkits for healing. The loadout excludes heavy weapons like the hammer or dynamite, because the focus is on survival and consistent progression rather than on fast wall-breaking.

The multi-floor loadout is the best choice for players who raid boss bases or other complex multi-floor structures. The high durability of the rope ensures you can complete long climbs without the rope breaking, and the dual medkits provide enough healing to survive multiple traps or falls.

## When to Upgrade Beyond Epic

The epic tier is the sweet spot for mid-game players, but it is not the endgame. The legendary and mythic tiers offer significantly higher damage values and more powerful special effects, but they are also much more expensive and harder to obtain. The optimal time to upgrade beyond epic is when you have fully upgraded all your epic gear to level 10 and when you have a consistent income that can afford the high cost of legendary upgrades.

For most players, the epic tier will last through the mid game and into the early late game. The transition to legendary gear typically happens when your account level is above 40 and when you have evolved several pets to legendary or mythic. At that point, the progression from epic to legendary becomes the next major goal.

## Common Epic Gear Mistakes

The most common epic gear mistake is upgrading epic gear too early. A new player obtains an epic pickaxe, immediately upgrades it to level 10, and then realizes they do not have enough cash for other essential upgrades, such as walls or traps. The fix is to upgrade epic gear gradually, alongside other investments, rather than pouring all resources into a single item.

The second most common mistake is using the wrong tool for the job. A player brings a hammer to a base that requires a pickaxe, or brings a rope to a single-floor base. The fix is to evaluate the base you are raiding and choose the appropriate tool for that specific base type. The general-purpose loadout is flexible, but it is not optimal for every situation.

The third most common mistake is ignoring the special effects of epic gear. An epic pickaxe with a combo damage effect is significantly more valuable than a rare pickaxe with higher base damage, but many players overlook the special effect and choose the item with the higher damage number instead. The fix is to read the item description carefully and prioritize special effects over raw damage numbers.

Epic gear is the workhorse of Build a Base and Steal. It is accessible, powerful, and versatile. Master the epic tier, and you will be well-prepared for the legendary and mythic tiers that follow.
