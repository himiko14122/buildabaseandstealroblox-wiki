## Understanding the Legendary Tier

Legendary gear in Build a Base and Steal is distinguished from lower tiers in three ways: power, utility, and exclusivity. Legendary items deal 3x to 5x the damage of basic weapons, which makes them the only realistic option for tackling the highest-tier raid targets. Finally, they are exclusive: the only ways to obtain legendary gear are through high-difficulty content or the in-game shop, which makes them a marker of long-term dedication rather than early-game luck.

The legendary tier is the most important progression milestone for end-game players, because it is the tier at which a player's gear starts to define their raid identity. ## Acquiring Legendary Gear

Legendary gear can be acquired through the in-game shop during special events, through the trading hub, or through limited-time event shops. Each acquisition method has its own tradeoffs. The in-game shop is the most reliable source, but the prices are high. Players who are saving for a specific legendary item should monitor the shop rotation and buy the item as soon as it appears.

The trading hub is a player-driven market where legendary gear can be found at lower prices than the in-game shop, but the supply is limited and the items are usually overpriced by sellers who know the value of their gear. Players who buy legendary gear from the trading hub need to be experienced traders who can identify good deals.

The event shop is a limited-time source of exclusive legendary gear that can only be obtained during events. Event-exclusive legendary gear is usually very strong, and it is often the best gear available in the game. Players who are serious about legendary gear should participate in every event that offers legendary rewards.

## Common Legendary Gear Mistakes

The most common legendary gear mistake is buying the wrong legendary item for your playstyle. A player who enjoys combat should buy the Legendary Energy Weapon, but a player who enjoys breaching should buy the Legendary Hammer. Buying a legendary item just because it is the strongest overall is a mistake, because the strongest item for your playstyle is more valuable than the strongest item overall.

The second most common legendary gear mistake is overpaying in the trading hub. The trading hub is full of sellers who know the value of their legendary items, and they will charge premium prices. A player who is not experienced at spotting fair trades will often overpay by 30 to 50 percent above the typical market rate, so it pays to learn the going rate for any item before you commit. The third most common mistake is saving for legendary gear without first upgrading the lower tiers. A player who is saving for the Legendary Blaster might use a basic blaster in the meantime, which is much less effective than the advanced blaster. The lower tiers of gear are not as good as legendary gear, but they are much better than basic gear, and a player who is serious about progression should upgrade to the advanced tier of every category before saving for legendary gear.
