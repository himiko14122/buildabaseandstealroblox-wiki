## Breaching Tools

Breaching tools are the most important category in Build a Base and Steal, and they are responsible for destroying walls and obstacles. Every base has walls, and every raid needs to break at least one wall to reach the loot. A player without breaching tools is forced to rely on their weapon to break walls, which is slow and inefficient.

The basic breaching charge is the most common breaching tool, and it is a one-use item that destroys a single wall in seconds. The advanced breaching charge destroys a larger area and can take out multiple walls at once. The legendary breaching charge is the most powerful and can clear a path through an entire wall section in a single use.

A player should always carry at least one breaching charge, even on raids that they expect to be easy. The cost of a breaching charge is small compared to the cost of a failed raid, and the breaching charge is often the difference between success and failure. Experienced players carry two or three breaching charges on every raid, to handle unexpected wall configurations.

### Scanner Tools

Scanner tools provide information about the target base, including the location of pets, the layout of walls, and the position of active defenders. Information is power in Build a Base and Steal, and a player with a scanner can make much better decisions about. The advanced scanner reveals the specific positions of high-value pets, which allows the player to plan their breach around the pet locations. The legendary scanner reveals the positions of all active defenders, which is invaluable for avoiding counter-attacks.

Scanners are particularly useful in counter-intelligence scenarios, where the target base has been modified to look like an easy target but is actually heavily defended. A scanner user will see through the disguise and avoid the trap, while a non-scanner user will walk in and get cleaned out.

The scanner has a few important limitations. It has a cooldown period between uses, it has a limited range, and it can be detected by the defending player, which warns them that an attack is coming. A skilled defender will set up a trap for attackers who use scanners, and the scanner user needs to be aware of this risk.

### Mobility Tools

Mobility tools enable the player to move around the base quickly, which is essential for both reaching the loot and escaping when the raid goes wrong. The most common mobility tools are grappling hooks, jetpacks, and speed boosts, each of which provides a different form of movement.

The grappling hook is a ranged tool that allows the player to quickly move to a specific location, such as a high wall or a distant platform. The grappling hook is particularly useful for bypassing walls and obstacles, and for reaching high reaching high platforms, and escaping from defenders. The jetpack is one of the most versatile mobility tools, but it consumes fuel quickly and is expensive to use.

The speed boost is a temporary buff that increases the player's movement speed for a few seconds. Speed boosts are useful for quick extractions, where the player needs to grab the loot and escape before defenders can respond. Speed boosts are also useful in counter-raids, where the defender needs to reach an attacking player quickly.

### Defense Tools

Defense tools are the utility items that help the player survive during a raid, and they include shields, healing items, and damage reduction buffs. Defense tools are not as exciting as weapons, but they are the difference between a raid that ends in extraction and a raid that ends in failure.

The basic shield absorbs damage for a few seconds and is the most common defense tool. The advanced shield absorbs more damage and lasts longer. The legendary shield provides a damage reduction buff that lasts for the entire raid, which is invaluable against heavily defended bases.

Healing items restore the player's health during a raid, which allows them to survive longer and complete more objectives. Healing items are particularly useful in long raids, where the player takes damage over time and needs to recover to continue fighting.

### Special Tools

Special tools are the niche items that provide unique abilities, such as the ability to teleport, the teleporter is a one-use item that allows the player to instantly move to a specific location within the base. The teleporter is useful for bypassing walls, escaping from defenders, and reaching high-value pets quickly. The teleporter is expensive, but it is the most powerful mobility tool in the game.

The defense disabler is a one-use item that temporarily disables a single trap or defense within the base. The defense disabler is useful for raiding heavily defended bases, and it is one of the few tools that can bypass a well-designed defense system.

The pet cloner is a special item that allows the player to create a copy of a pet they have stolen, which is useful for players who want to grow their collection without rolling. The pet cloner is a niche item, but it can be a major asset for traders and collectors.

## Building a Tool Loadout

A balanced tool loadout should include at least one breaching tool, one scanner tool, one mobility tool, and one defense tool. The specific items depend on your playstyle, your team composition, and the type of raid you are attempting.

A solo raid loadout should prioritize breaching and mobility, because the solo player needs to break into the base and escape quickly without backup. A team raid loadout should include more support-oriented tools, because the team can coordinate their efforts and cover each other's weaknesses.

The tool loadout should also be flexible. Some raids are easier than others, and a loadout that is designed for tough raids will be overkill for easy raids. Experienced players switch their loadout based on the target base, The basic version is the starter, the advanced version is the mid-game goal, and the legendary version is the end-game goal.

A reasonable tool upgrade budget is to spend 30 percent of your tool cash on breaching, 25 percent on scanners, 25 percent on mobility, and 20 percent on defense. This split gives you a balanced tool loadout that can handle most raid scenarios, and it leaves room for occasional use of special tools.

The upgrade priority is usually breaching first, because breaching is the most important tool category. The second priority is scanners, because information is the foundation of good decisions. The third priority is mobility, because mobility enables the rest of the loadout. The fourth priority is defense, because defense is important but not as critical as the other categories.

Always check your breaching charges before starting a raid, and bring more than you think you need.
