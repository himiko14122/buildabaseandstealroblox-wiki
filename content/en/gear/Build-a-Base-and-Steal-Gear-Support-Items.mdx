Support items are the unsung heroes of any Build a Base and Steal loadout. While weapons deal the damage and tools enable the damage, support items keep the player alive long enough to use both. Support items in Build a Base and Steal serve three main purposes: damage absorption, health restoration, and survival utility. Damage absorption items like shields reduce the damage the player takes during combat, health restoration items like healing packs restore the player's health after taking damage, and survival utility items like escape tools provide ways to get out of dangerous situations.

The most common beginner mistake is to invest everything in weapons and tools, and to ignore support items entirely. The result is a loadout that can deal a lot of damage but cannot survive a counter-attack. Experienced players know that a balanced loadout is the key to long-term raiding success, and they invest at least 25 percent of their gear budget in support items.

The right support items can completely change the outcome of a raid. A player with a strong shield can survive a counter-attack that would have killed a player without a shield. A player with a strong healing pack can recover from a near-death experience and continue the raid. A player with a strong escape tool can withdraw from a failed raid and try again later, rather than losing everything.

## Shield Categories

Shields are the most common support item, and they absorb damage for a limited duration. There are three shield types: damage shields, damage reduction shields, and reflection shields. Each type provides a different defensive benefit, and the is the starter shield. The advanced damage shield absorbs more damage, and the legendary damage shield absorbs enough damage to survive multiple hits from a powerful attacker.

The damage reduction shield is a more advanced shield type that reduces all incoming damage by a fixed percentage, rather than absorbing a fixed amount. The damage reduction shield is more effective against sustained attacks, and it is the shield of choice for players who expect to be in combat for an extended period.

The reflection shield is a special shield type that reflects a portion of the damage back to the attacker. The reflection shield is a niche item, but it can be devastating in the right scenario, particularly against attackers who rely on rapid-fire weapons. A skilled reflection shield user can defeat much stronger attackers by forcing them to take their own damage.

### Healing Items

Healing items restore the player's health during a raid, which allows them to survive longer and complete more objectives. There are three healing item types: instant healing, over-time healing, and revival items. Each type provides a different healing benefit, and the right item depends on the raid scenario.

The instant healing item restores a fixed amount of health immediately upon use. The instant healing item is the most common healing item, and it is the go-to choice for most raid scenarios. The basic instant healing item restores a small amount of health, while the advanced and legendary versions restore progressively more.

The over-time healing item restores health gradually over a few seconds, which is useful in scenarios where the player is taking sustained damage. The over-time healing item is more efficient than the instant healing item for sustained damage, but it is less effective for emergency healing. Revival items are expensive, but they are invaluable in tough raids.

### Escape Tools

Escape tools allow the player to withdraw from a raid when things go wrong, which is essential for preserving the loot they have already collected. The most common escape tools are teleporters, cloaking devices, and speed boosts.

The teleporter is a one-use item that allows the player to instantly move to a specific location, usually back to their own base. The teleporter is the most powerful escape tool, and it can save the player from almost any situation. The basic teleporter has a long cooldown, while the advanced and legendary versions have shorter cooldowns.

The cloaking device makes the player invisible to other players for a few seconds, which allows them to escape from combat or bypass defenders. The cloaking device is a niche tool, but it is invaluable in scenarios where the player needs to escape from a superior force.

The speed boost is a temporary buff that increases the player's movement speed, which allows them to outrun defenders and escape from combat. The speed boost is the cheapest escape tool, and it is a good choice for players who are on a budget.

### Building a Support Loadout

A balanced support loadout should include at least one shield, one healing item, and one escape tool. The specific items depend on your playstyle, your team composition, and the type of raid you are attempting.

A solo raid loadout should prioritize shields and healing items, because the solo player does not have teammates to back them up. A team raid loadout should include more escape tools their loadout based on the target base, and they save the expensive support items for the raids that actually need them.

### Support Item Upgrade Path

The support item upgrade path follows the same structure as the weapon and tool upgrade paths. Each support item comes in three rarity tiers, and you should progress through them in order.

A reasonable support item upgrade budget is to spend 40 percent on shields, 30 percent on healing items, and 30 percent on escape tools. This split gives you a balanced support loadout that can handle most raid scenarios, and it leaves room for occasional use of special support items like revival items.

The upgrade priority is usually shields first, because shields are the most important support item category. The second priority is healing items, because healing is essential for survival. The third priority is escape tools, because escape tools are useful but not as critical as shields and healing.

## Common Support Item Mistakes

The most common support item mistake is forgetting to bring support items at all. A player who goes on a raid without any support items is essentially gambling that they will not take any damage, which is almost never the case. Always check your support items before starting a raid, and bring at least one shield, one healing item, and one escape tool.

The second most common support item mistake is using healing items at the wrong time. Healing items restore the player's health, but they also have a casting time during which the player is vulnerable. A player who uses a healing item in the middle of a firefight is likely to be defeated before the healing completes. The right time to use a healing item is when the player is out of combat, such as after a successful breach or after a successful escape.

The third most common support item mistake is not using the shield at all. Many players buy a shield but never use it, because they think of the shield as a "panic button" rather than a tool. The shield is most effective when it is used to use the shield.

## Long-Term Support Item Strategy

A long-term support item strategy should include a mix of consumable and reusable support items. Consumable support items are one-use items that provide powerful effects, while reusable support items are items that can be used multiple times but provide weaker effects. The mix of consumable and reusable support items depends on your budget and your playstyle.

Players with a limited budget should focus on reusable support items, because they provide more value per cost over time. Players with a larger budget should invest in both consumable and reusable support items, using the consumable items for the toughest raids and the reusable items for everyday play.

The long-term goal is to have a full legendary support loadout, which provides the maximum survival benefit in every raid scenario. The full legendary support loadout is expensive and takes months to acquire, but it is the foundation of the most successful end-game raiding strategies.
