Rebirthing in **Build** a Base and Steal is the long-term progression mechanic that separates committed players from casual players. Each rebirth resets your base, your cash, and your pet roster, but grants a permanent prestige multiplier that applies to all future income. The decision of when to rebirth, what to preserve, and how to optimize each cycle is the strategic core of the endgame. This guide breaks down the optimal rebirth strategies, from the first rebirth to the twentieth, and shows you how to maximize your prestige multiplier over time.

## The First Rebirth: Timing and Preparation

The first rebirth is a milestone, but it is also a trap for impatient players. The optimal first rebirth occurs when you have reached account level 30, when you have a pet roster of 20-25 pets with at least 5 legendaries, and when you have exhausted the progression options in your current server. Rebirthing before these conditions are met will slow your progression rather than accelerate it, because the prestige multiplier is small and the reset wipes out weeks of progress.

The preparation for the first rebirth begins weeks in advance. You should have 5 favorite pets marked, including your highest-rarity pets and your most valuable skill pets. You should have a stockpile of evolution materials for post-rebirth pets. You should have a clear plan for your post-rebirth base layout, including the placement of auras and happiness objects. Entering the rebirth with a plan ensures that you do not waste the early rebirth cycles rebuilding from scratch.

### The 5-Favorite System
The 5-favorite system is the most important tool for preserving progress through a rebirth. Before initiating a rebirth, mark your 5 highest-rarity pets as favorites. These pets carry over with their full level, friendship, and effectiveness. The 5-pet limit is tight, and choosing which pets to preserve is a strategic decision.

The optimal favorite selection for the first rebirth is: your highest-rarity offensive pet, your highest-rarity defensive pet, your highest-rarity utility pet, and your two highest-rarity pets overall. This selection gives you a strong foundation for the post-rebirth base, with coverage across all three skill categories. For subsequent rebirths, you may adjust the selection based on your current build and upcoming events.

## The Rebirth Cycle: Early, Mid, and Late

### Early Cycle (Rebirths 1-5)
The early rebirth cycle focuses on building a strong pet roster quickly. The prestige multiplier during this cycle is small (1.10-1.30), so the income boost from the multiplier is not yet significant. The priority is to rebuild your roster to 20-25 pets, with a focus on high-rarity pets and skill pets. The early cycle is also the time to experiment with different build configurations and identify which pets work best for your playstyle.

The optimal strategy for the early cycle is to roll heavily during events, to prioritize pets with percentage-based special abilities, and to evolve your favorite pets to the highest rarity possible. The early cycle is also the time to upgrade your gear to epic rarity, because the gear upgrade is permanent and provides value in every subsequent rebirth.

### Mid Cycle (Rebirths 6-15)
The mid rebirth cycle focuses on maximizing the value of your prestige multiplier. The prestige multiplier during this cycle is significant (1.30-2.00), and the income boost from the multiplier is a major contributor to your total income. The priority is to optimize your base for maximum offline income, because the multiplier applies to both active and passive income.

The optimal strategy for the mid cycle is to refine your aura placement, to maximize happiness on all pets, and to transition your roster to legendary pets. The mid cycle is also the time to upgrade your gear to legendary rarity, because the legendary gear makes raids significantly faster and allows you to obtain more pets in less time.

### Late Cycle (Rebirths 16+)
The late rebirth cycle focuses on reaching the prestige plateau. The prestige multiplier during this cycle is very high (2.00+), and the marginal benefit of additional rebirths decreases. The priority is to build a stable, optimized base that generates maximum passive income, rather than chasing additional rebirths.

The optimal strategy for the late cycle is to evolve your pets to mythic, to craft mythic gear, and to refine your base configuration to perfection. The late cycle is also the time to focus on raiding for materials and crafting mythic items, because the material grind is the bottleneck at this stage.

## Rebirth Timing: When to Reset

The decision of when to rebirth depends on three factors: your current prestige multiplier, your roster strength, and the upcoming events. The optimal rebirth timing is when you have plateaued in your current cycle, when your roster is strong enough to rebuild quickly after the reset, and when there are no major events in the next 7 days.

The plateau point is when your daily income has been flat for a week, despite active play. This indicates that you have exhausted the progression options in your current cycle, and a reset will allow you to rebuild with the prestige multiplier compounding your income. The roster strength point is when you have 20-25 pets, with at least 10 legendaries. This ensures that you can rebuild a strong roster within 3-5 days after the rebirth.

### The Event Consideration
Events are a major factor in rebirth timing. Rebirthing right before a major event is generally optimal, because the prestige multiplier compounds with the event bonus for the entire event window. A player who rebirths the day before a 2x event can collect double-event income from the very first hour, which is a substantial head start over a player who rebirthed a week earlier.

The exception is for rolling events. During a rolling event, the optimal strategy is to delay the rebirth until after the event, because the event increases the drop rate for pets, and you want to take advantage of the event before resetting. The optimal rebirth timing depends on the event type, and players should check the event calendar before making a decision.

## Rebirth Bonuses: Maximizing Multiplier Gains

The prestige multiplier increases by 0.05-0.10x per rebirth, depending on the conditions met at the time of the reset. The conditions include: reaching a specific pet count, holding a mythic at the time of reset, having no losses during the run, and completing the rebirth during an event. Meeting multiple conditions stacks the bonuses, so the optimal rebirth meets as many conditions as possible.

The pet count condition requires reaching 25 pets before the rebirth. The mythic condition requires holding at least one mythic pet at the time of reset. The no-losses condition requires completing the entire run without ever failing a raid or losing a pet. The event condition requires completing the rebirth during a themed event. The optimal rebirth meets all four conditions, which maximizes the multiplier gain.

Over multiple rebirths, this difference compounds into a significant gap in the prestige multiplier.

## Rebirth and the Offline Income Multiplier

The prestige multiplier applies to offline income, which makes rebirth timing even more important. A player with a 1.5x prestige multiplier generates 50% more offline income than a player with no multiplier, even if both players have identical pets and identical base configurations. The difference compounds over time, and the player with the higher prestige multiplier will eventually reach the offline cap faster.

The optimal offline strategy for a high-prestige player is to log in more frequently than a low-prestige player. A player with a 1.5x multiplier hits the cap in 16 hours instead of 24, so logging in every 12-16 hours maximizes offline income. A player with a 2.0x multiplier hits the cap in 12 hours, so logging in every 8-12 hours maximizes offline income.

### The Premium Cap Interaction
The premium 72-hour cap extension is especially valuable for high-prestige players. A player with a 1.5x multiplier can collect 72 hours of accumulation in 48 hours, which is 1.5x the income of a non-premium player. A player with a 2.0x multiplier can collect 72 hours of accumulation in 36 hours, which is 2x the income of a non-premium player. The combination of high prestige and premium cap is the most powerful offline configuration in the game.

## Common Rebirth Mistakes

The most common rebirth mistake is rebirthing too early. A player reaches account level 15 and immediately rebirths, expecting the prestige multiplier to accelerate their progression. In reality, the multiplier is small and the reset wipes out too much progress. The fix is to wait until account level 30 and a strong roster before rebirthing.

The second most common mistake is not marking favorites. A player rebirths without marking any favorites, and all of their high-rarity pets are lost. The fix is to always mark 5 favorites before initiating a rebirth, and to choose those favorites carefully based on your long-term build.

The third most common mistake is ignoring event timing. A player rebirths the day before a rolling event, missing the increased drop rate for pets. The fix is to check the event calendar and time rebirths around events, rather than rebirthing randomly.

Rebirthing is the ultimate long-term progression mechanic in Build a Base and Steal. Master the rebirth cycle, optimize your timing, and you will build a prestige multiplier that accelerates every aspect of your game.
