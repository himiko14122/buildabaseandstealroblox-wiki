Aggressive strategies in Build a Base and Steal are about overwhelming defenses through brute force. Instead of carefully navigating trap fields or patiently picking at weak points, the aggressive raider charges straight through, using superior gear and overwhelming damage to destroy everything in their path. This approach is faster, more exciting, and more profitable in the short term, but it also carries higher risks and requires significant investment in gear and skill. This guide breaks down the aggressive playstyle, explains the gear and techniques required, and shows you how to dominate servers through pure combat power.

## The Aggressive Mindset

The aggressive mindset is about speed and force. You accept that you will take damage, but you calculate that the time you save by charging through outweighs the cost of healing. You accept that you may fail occasionally, but you calculate that the profits from successful raids outweigh the losses from failures. The aggressive raider plays on the edge, constantly pushing the limits of what their gear can handle.

The aggressive mindset also involves a different relationship with risk. The defensive raider minimizes risk at all costs, even if it means slower progress. The aggressive raider embraces risk, because risk is the price of speed. The optimal aggressive raider knows exactly how much risk their gear can handle, and they push right up to that limit without exceeding it.

### When Aggressive Is Optimal
Aggressive strategies are optimal when you have superior gear, when the base you are raiding has weak defenses, and when you need to complete raids quickly. If your gear is significantly stronger than the base's defenses, charging through is faster than careful navigation. If the base has sparse traps, you can afford to trigger them rather than avoiding them. If you are farming multiple bases in a short session, speed is more important than caution.

Aggressive strategies are also optimal during events, when the event bonus multiplies the value of raids. Completing more raids in the event window maximizes the event bonus, and aggressive raiding is the fastest way to complete raids. The aggressive raider can complete 2-3 raids in the time it takes a careful raider to complete 1 raid, which means they earn 2-3x the event bonus.

## Aggressive Raiding Techniques

### The Charge Technique
The charge technique is the core of aggressive raiding. Instead of carefully avoiding traps, you charge straight through them, triggering them intentionally and relying on your healing to keep you alive. The charge technique is faster than careful navigation, because you do not spend time analyzing trap placement or planning routes.

The optimal charge technique involves identifying the densest cluster of traps and charging through the center of the cluster. The center of the cluster typically has the highest concentration of traps, but it is also the shortest path through the cluster. By charging through the center, you minimize the time spent in the trap field, even though you trigger more traps.

### The Bypass Technique
The bypass technique is a complementary technique to the charge technique. Instead of breaking through every wall, you use the Mythic Rope to bypass walls entirely by climbing over them. The rope allows you to climb any surface, including walls and ceilings, which means you can skip entire layers of defenses.

The optimal bypass technique involves identifying walls that are thick or heavily reinforced, and using the rope to climb over them instead of breaking through them. The rope is faster than breaking through thick walls, and it also avoids triggering traps that are placed near those walls. The bypass technique is especially valuable in multi-floor bases, where climbing vertically is often faster than breaking through horizontal walls.

### The Chain Explosion Technique
The chain explosion technique is an advanced aggressive technique that uses the Mythic Dynamite to trigger chain reactions of trap explosions. The technique involves placing dynamite in the center of a trap cluster, which triggers the traps, which in turn triggers adjacent traps, creating a chain reaction that clears the entire cluster.

The optimal chain explosion technique requires precise placement of the dynamite. The dynamite must be placed at the geometric center of the trap cluster, where it will trigger the maximum number of traps with the initial explosion. The chain reaction then spreads outward, clearing the entire cluster. The technique is difficult to master, but it is extremely fast when executed correctly.

## Aggressive Base Building

### The Raid Magnet Base
The raid magnet base is designed to attract raids, because the aggressive player prefers to be raided rather than to do the raiding. The base has minimal defenses, with a single layer of weak walls and few traps, but it has a high-value inner layer with valuable pets. The base is designed to be easily breached, but it is also designed to minimize the loss when it is breached.

The optimal raid magnet base places the valuable pets in a compact inner layer, surrounded by a single layer of weak walls. The base has few auras and few happiness objects, to minimize the value of the pets that are lost during a raid. The aggressive player accepts that they will lose pets regularly, but they calculate that the profits from their own raids outweigh the losses from being raided.

### The Counter-Raid Base
The counter-raid base is designed to punish aggressive raiders. The base has multiple layers of thick walls and dense trap clusters, which slow down the aggressive raider and maximize the damage they take. The base is designed to extract a high cost from the raider, even if the raider eventually succeeds.

The optimal counter-raid base uses overlapping trap coverage and funneling walls to force the raider into kill zones. The base also includes defensive pets and defensive auras, which increase the damage output of the traps. The goal is not necessarily to prevent the raid, but to make the raid so expensive that the raider thinks twice before raiding again.

## Aggressive Server Domination

### The Alpha Raider
The alpha raider is the aggressive player who dominates a server through sheer force. The alpha raider raids constantly, targets the weakest bases first, and builds a reputation that discourages other players from raiding their own base. The alpha raider is not necessarily the strongest player in the server, but they are the most active and the most ruthless.

The optimal alpha raider strategy involves raiding every base in the server at least once, to establish dominance. The alpha raider then focuses on farming the weakest bases repeatedly, because the profit-per-hour is highest against weak bases. The alpha raider also defends their own base aggressively, raiding back any player who raids them, to maintain their reputation.

### The Server Hopper
The server hopper is the aggressive player who moves between servers, farming each server for a few hours before moving on. The server hopper does not establish a permanent base in any server, but instead creates a temporary base, farms aggressively, and then moves on when the server becomes too difficult or too competitive.

The optimal server hopper strategy involves researching servers before joining, to identify servers with many weak bases and few strong raiders. The server hopper joins the server, creates a temporary base, farms aggressively for 4-6 hours, and then moves on. The server hopper maximizes their profit-per-hour by constantly farming the most profitable servers.

## Common Aggressive Mistakes

The most common aggressive mistake is overestimating your gear. A player with rare gear attempts an aggressive charge through a base with legendary defenses, and they are wiped out before reaching the inner layer. The fix is to accurately assess your gear's strength relative to the base's defenses, and to adjust your aggression accordingly.

The second most common mistake is ignoring the healing cost. A player charges through trap fields without considering the cost of healing, and they end up spending more on healing than they earn from the raid. The fix is to calculate the healing cost before the raid, and to ensure that the profit exceeds the cost.

The third most common mistake is burning out. A player raids aggressively for hours without taking breaks, and they make mistakes due to fatigue. The fix is to take regular breaks and to maintain focus, because aggressive raiding requires constant attention and quick reflexes.

Aggressive strategies are the fastest way to progress in Build a Base and Steal, but they are also the riskiest. Master the aggressive playstyle, and you will dominate servers and earn massive profits, but you must be prepared for the occasional failure that comes with playing on the edge.
