## How the Tier List Is Built

The tier list below ranks pets based on three weighted criteria: raw income rate, collection bonus value, and trading liquidity. Raw income rate is the cash a pet generates per hour, before any multipliers. Collection bonus value is the additional income or special effect you receive for completing a set of related pets. Trading liquidity is how easy it is to buy or sell the pet in the trading hub, which determines its real-world usefulness as a trading asset.

A pet that scores well on all three criteria lands in the S tier. A pet that scores well on one or two but poorly on the rest lands in A pet's design or aesthetic value; it is purely a strategic ranking for players trying to optimize their rolling budget.

The list also includes a "collection priority" recommendation, which is separate from the tier ranking. A pet might be S tier for income but low priority for collection if its collection bonus is weak. Conversely, a pet might be B tier for income but high priority for collection if its bonus is exceptional. Treat the two ratings as separate inputs to your rolling decisions, not as a single combined score.

## Adapting the Tier List to Your Playstyle

The tier list is a starting point, not a final answer. Different playstyles value different pets. A player who is focused on trading values liquidity more than income rate, and should weight the list accordingly. A player who is focused on offline income values raw income rate above all else, and should weight the list in the opposite direction. A player who is focused on collection bonuses values set completion above individual pet quality, and should use the list as a guide to which sets to prioritize.

The tier list also changes with each patch. Buffs and nerfs can move pets up or down several tiers in a single update. The pet that is S tier today might be B, read the patch notes carefully, and be ready to adjust your rolling targets whenever the meta shifts.

Finally, remember that the tier list is a tool, not a rule. If you love a particular pet regardless of its tier ranking, roll for it. The game is supposed to be fun, and fun is the most important optimization target of all. The tier list is for players who want to maximize their progression efficiency, and it is a great tool for that purpose, but it is not the only way to play Build a Base and Steal.
