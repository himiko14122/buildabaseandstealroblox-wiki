## Daily Login Rewards

The most reliable free reward is the **daily login bonus**, which grants throughout the month: day 1 grants a small cash bonus, day 7 grants a free roll, day 14 grants a larger cash bonus plus a free roll, day 21 grants a rare or better pet, and day 28 grants a substantial cash bundle plus an exclusive cosmetic. Missing a day resets your streak, so consistent logins matter.

The optimal strategy is to log in at least once per day, even if you only have time to claim the bonus and log out. Five minutes is enough to preserve your streak. Players who skip a day lose their progress back to day 1, which is a significant loss compared to the modest time investment required to maintain the streak. Set a daily reminder on your phone if you are prone to forgetting.

The 28-day cycle repeats, so even after you hit day 28, the cycle restarts at day 1. The second cycle's rewards are the same as the first, so this is a renewable free reward that compounds indefinitely. Players who maintain their daily login streak for a full year earn thousands of dollars worth of in-game value from this system alone.

### Maximizing Login Streak Value

To get the most out of daily login rewards, time your highest-value sessions around the milestone days. Day 7, 14, 21, and 28 are the most session for the milestone days so you can claim the bonus and immediately invest the rewards.

Another tip: avoid logging in right before the daily reset time. If the daily reset is at midnight UTC and you log in at 11:55 PM, you only get 5 minutes of streak time before the reset, but the timer treats it as a new day. Logging in at 12:05 AM gives you a full 24-hour window. This trick can help you align your streak with your real-life schedule.

## Event Rewards

The developers run **limited-time events** roughly every two to four weeks, each tied to a theme like base defense, raiding, pets, or seasonal celebrations. Events typically grant free rewards through three channels: a participation reward (given to anyone who logs in during the event), a milestone reward (given when you complete specific tasks during the event), and a leaderboard reward (given to top performers in the event's competitive activity).

Participation rewards are usually small but guaranteed, often a few hundred cash plus a free roll. Milestone rewards are more substantial and require you to complete specific tasks like rolling 50 times during the event or the top spots. The combination of all three makes events a major free reward source, and players who actively engage with events earn substantially more free value than those who ignore them.

Check the events tab in the main menu at the start of every session to see what is currently active. The events tab displays the duration, the rewards, and the tasks required for each milestone. Planning your play sessions around event tasks is the easiest way to compound your rewards.

### Event Stacking

Some events overlap, and the developers occasionally run two events simultaneously. When this happens, you can complete tasks for both events in the same play session, effectively doubling your reward output. For example, if there is a "roll 50 times" event and a "raid 10 bases" event running at the same time, every roll you do and every raid you complete counts toward both milestones. Stacking events is the single most efficient way to maximize free rewards, and players who learn to recognize stacking opportunities progress much faster than players who treat each event in isolation.

## Achievement Milestones

The **achievement system** grants one-time rewards for completing specific in-game milestones, such as reaching 10 pets, building 50 walls, completing your first raid, a meaningful boost at each progression stage.

Most achievements grant cash, but a few grant exclusive items, including rare pets that cannot be obtained any other way. To see the full achievement list, open the menu and navigate to the Achievements tab. Each achievement shows its current status (locked or unlocked), the reward, and a progress bar. Working through the achievement list is a great way to ensure you have not missed any free rewards, especially if you have been playing casually and not paying close attention to the system.

## Social Media Giveaways

The **official X account** and the **official Discord** run periodic giveaways that grant free in-game rewards, including rare pets, large cash bundles, and exclusive cosmetics. Giveaways typically require you to like, retweet, or comment on a post, and winners are chosen randomly. The odds of winning are low, but the rewards are significant, and the time investment is minimal.

Beyond the official giveaways, **partner creators** run their own giveaways through their YouTube channels, Discord servers, and X accounts. These giveaways are more frequent and often have better odds because the entry pools are smaller. Following a handful of reputable creators and entering their giveaways is a low-effort way to capture occasional high-value rewards.

The key to making social media giveaways worthwhile is volume. Entering one giveaway gives you a small chance of winning. Entering ten giveaways gives you a much better chance. Spend 15 minutes each week entering every active giveaway, and over a year, you will win several meaningful rewards.

### Avoiding Giveaway Scams

Not every giveaway is legitimate. Scammers impersonate the official accounts for your password, never require you to click a suspicious link, and never ask for payment information. If a giveaway asks for any of these things, it is a scam.

Verify the account running the giveaway by checking for the official verification badge, the follower count, and the account age. Real official accounts have been active for years and have thousands of followers. Scam accounts are usually created within the last few weeks and have very few followers. When in doubt, check the official Discord for an announcement about the giveaway. If the giveaway is not mentioned on the official Discord, it is not real.
