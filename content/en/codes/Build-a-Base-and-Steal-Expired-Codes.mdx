### Why Some Codes Are Marked "Retired Silently"

A small number of codes are pulled without an announcement because the developers notice a problem shortly after release. Common reasons include a reward that would inflate the in-game economy if widely redeemed, or a code that conflicts with another live code. When this happens, the developers disable the code and may or may not post about it. We mark these as "retired silently" and do our best to identify the cause after the fact.

Players who redeemed a silently retired code generally keep their rewards, because retroactively clawing back rewards would damage trust. The developers prefer to retire a problematic code quickly and accept the small economy impact rather than punish the players who redeemed it in good faith. This is the right approach, and it is one reason the community trusts the live-ops team.
