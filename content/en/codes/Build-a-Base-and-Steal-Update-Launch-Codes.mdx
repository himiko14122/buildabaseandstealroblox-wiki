## The Update Code Pattern

Every major Build a Base and Steal update follows a predictable code pattern. The pattern starts with a "pre-launch" code, usually dropped 24 to 48 hours before the update goes live, which grants a small reward as a teaser. The pattern continues with 2 to 3 "launch day" codes, dropped within the first few hours of the update, which are the most generous and have the shortest active windows. The pattern concludes with 1 to 2 "extended" codes, dropped 1 to 2 weeks after the launch, which give stragglers a chance to claim rewards.

The pre-launch code is usually cash-only, with a modest amount (1,000 to 2,500). The launch day codes are the main event, and they typically include a mix of cash, free rolls, and one premium reward (like a free legendary roll or a double income boost). The extended codes are usually a repeat of one of the launch day rewards, granted to players who missed the initial window.

## Strategy for Update Codes

The optimal strategy for update codes is to redeem them on launch day, even if you are not sure what the rewards are. Update codes have the shortest active windows of any code type, and the most valuable ones often expire within a week. Redeeming on launch day gives you the full window to use the rewards, and it also gives you the time-limited boosts at the start of your update play session.

The second strategy is to redeem the time-limited boosts first and the permanent items second. The time-limited boosts (like the 2x offline income) are most valuable when you can use them during a planned play session, not at a random moment. The permanent items do not have this constraint, so they can be redeemed whenever you have a free moment.

The third strategy is to share the update codes with friends who may have missed them. Some update codes have a per-account limit, but most do not, and the generosity of sharing builds social capital that pays off in the long run through the community's help in return.
