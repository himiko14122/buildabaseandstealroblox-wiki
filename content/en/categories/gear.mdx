## 🔧 Understanding Gear Systems

### The Importance of Equipment

Gear is the **tactical backbone** of Build a Base and Steal, determining your effectiveness in both offensive raiding and defensive operations. The right equipment can mean the difference between successful raids and costly failures, or between an impenetrable base and easy prey for raiders.

### Equipment Categories Overview

#### Raiding Equipment
- **Purpose**: Offensive tools for breaching bases and stealing pets


- **Focus**: Speed, stealth, and overwhelming force


- **Investment**: High ongoing cost for consumables and repairs


- **Return**: Potential for high-value loot and resource acquisition

#### Defensive Equipment
- **Purpose**: Tools and items for protecting your base


- **Focus**: Durability, detection, and damage mitigation


- **Investment**: One-time purchases with maintenance costs


- **Return**: Asset protection and deterrence of raiders

#### Utility Equipment
- **Purpose**: General purpose equipment for various situations


- **Focus**: Versatility and convenience


- **Investment**: Moderate costs for多功能 items


- **Return**: Improved efficiency and capabilities across all activities

## ⚔️ Raiding Equipment

### Breaching Tools

#### Break-in Tools

##### Breach Hammers
- **Function**: Raw damage against walls and doors


- **Effectiveness**: High damage, slow attack speed


- **Cost**: Moderate initial cost, low per-use cost


- **Best Against**: Reinforced walls and strong doors


- **Strategy**: Use for concentrated breakthrough points

##### Cutting Tools
- **Function**: Silent entry through barriers


- **Effectiveness**: Medium damage, medium speed


- **Cost**: Low initial cost, moderate per-use cost


- **Best Against**: Chain link fences, wooden barriers


- **Strategy**: Stealthy approach when noise is a concern

##### Explosive Charges
- **Function**: Maximum damage for quick access


- **Effectiveness**: Very high damage, instant effect


- **Cost**: High initial cost, very high per-use cost


- **Best Against**: Multiple barriers simultaneously


- **Strategy**: Emergency situations or time-critical raids

##### Drilling Equipment
- **Function**: Precision entry through reinforced materials


- **Effectiveness**: High damage, slow but quiet


- **Cost**: High initial cost, moderate per-use cost


- **Best Against**: Metal doors and reinforced structures


- **Strategy**: Precision breaches when stealth matters

#### Access Tools

##### Lock Picks
- **Function**: Stealthy entry through locked containers


- **Effectiveness**: Silent success, skill-dependent


- **Cost**: Low initial cost, consumable use


- **Best Against**: Standard locks and containers


- **Strategy**: Primary tool for stealth operations

##### Hacking Devices
- **Function**: Electronic access to security systems


- **Effectiveness**: Can bypass advanced security


- **Cost**: High initial cost, power consumption


- **Best Against**: Electronic locks and security systems


- **Strategy**: High-tech bases with electronic defenses

##### Key Cloning
- **Function**: Duplicate keys for repeat access


- **Effectiveness**: Permanent access once cloned


- **Cost**: Moderate initial cost, per-key cost


- **Best Against**: When planning multiple raids on same target


- **Strategy**: Long-term raiding campaigns against specific targets

##### Bypass Tools
- **Function**: Circumvent specific security measures


- **Effectiveness**: Specialized counter to specific defenses


- **Cost**: Varies by specific tool type


- **Best Against**: Known vulnerabilities in target defenses


- **Strategy**: Exploiting known weaknesses in target bases

#### Assault Loadout
**Purpose**: Maximum offensive capability for quick raids
- **Primary**: Breach hammers, explosive charges

- **Primary**: Fortification tools, defensive structures
