## 🏰 Understanding Base Defense Principles

### The Defense Philosophy
Base defense is the **critical protective component** of Build a Base and Steal. A well-designed base serves multiple purposes:

#### Primary Objectives
- **Asset Protection**: Safeguard your valuable pets and resources

- **Deterrence**: Make raiding your base too costly or difficult

- **Loss Minimization**: Reduce damage when successful raids occur

- **Cost Efficiency**: Provide maximum protection for reasonable investment

### Core Defense Concepts

#### Layered Defense Strategy
```
Outer Detection → Outer Barriers → Inner Defenses → Core Protection → Emergency Measures
```

#### The Defense Equation
```
Base Security = (Structural Strength + Active Defenses + Detection Systems) / (Predictability + Accessibility)
```

### Defense vs. Cost Balance

#### Investment Principles
- **Diminishing Returns**: Each additional layer provides less benefit

- **Threat-Based Spending**: Match defense investment to asset value

- **Progressive Enhancement**: Improve defenses as your collection grows

- **Opportunity Cost**: Balance defense spending with other priorities
