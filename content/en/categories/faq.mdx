Find quick answers to the most **frequently asked questions** about Build a Base and Steal. Our comprehensive FAQ covers everything from basic gameplay to advanced strategies.

## General Questions

### What is Build a Base and Steal?
Build a Base and Steal is a Roblox simulation game that combines pet collection, base building, and strategic raiding. Players roll for pets, build defensive bases, and raid other players to steal their pets.

### Is Build a Base and Steal free to play?
Yes, Build a Base and Steal is completely free to play on Roblox. However, there may be optional in-game purchases available.

### What are the system requirements?
Build a Base and Steal runs on any device that can run Roblox, including PC, mobile devices, and tablets.

### How do I start playing?
Simply search for "Build a Base and Steal" on Roblox and click the play button. No additional downloads or installations are required.

## Gameplay Questions

### How do I get pets?
Pets are primarily obtained through rolling, which costs in-game currency. You can also trade with other players or obtain special pets during events.

### What is offline income?
Offline income is cash generated by your pets even when you're not actively playing. The amount depends on your pets' rarity and how long you've been away.

### How do I build a base?
Access the build mode from the game menu, select building materials, and place them to create your base. Focus on creating layers of defense to protect your pets.

### Can I play with friends?
Yes! Build a Base and Steal supports private servers where you can play with friends. You can also join public servers with up to 6 players.

## Progression Questions

### What's the best way to progress quickly?
Focus on rolling for high-income pets early, build basic defenses, then expand your collection and improve your base over time.

### How do I get more money?
Money is generated by your pets over time. Focus on acquiring higher rarity pets and optimizing your collection for maximum income.

### What should I prioritize as a beginner?
As a beginner, prioritize getting your first pets, building basic defenses, and understanding the core game mechanics before investing heavily in any one area.

### How long does it take to become good at the game?
Most players become comfortable with the basics within a few days, but mastering advanced strategies can take weeks or months of gameplay.

## Technical Questions

Yes, Build a Base and Steal is available on mobile devices through the Roblox app.

### Is there cross-platform play?
Yes, you can play with players on different platforms as long as you're all on Roblox.

## Codes and Rewards

### How do I redeem codes?
Find the codes button in the game menu, enter the code exactly as shown, and click redeem to claim your rewards.

## Trading and Economy

### Can I trade with other players?
Yes, trading is a core part of Build a Base and Steal. You can trade pets, resources, and items with other players.

### How do I know what something is worth?
Pet and item values fluctuate based on supply and demand. Check our [Pets List](/pets-list) and community discussions for current value information.

### Is there real money trading?
No, real money trading is not supported and is against Roblox's terms of service. All trading should be done with in-game items and currency.

### How do I avoid getting scammed?
Only trade with reputable players, use official trading systems when available, and never give away your items without receiving promised items in return.

## Raiding and Defense

### How do I raid other players?
Equip raiding gear, select a target base, and execute your raid strategy. Different bases require different approaches based on their defenses.

### What happens if someone raids me?
If your base is successfully raided, you may lose pets and resources. However, you can build defenses to make raiding difficult and minimize losses.

### Can I protect my base completely?
While no base is completely unraidable, you can build very strong defenses that deter most raiders and minimize potential losses.

### What's the best defense strategy?
Layer your defenses, use traps and surveillance systems, and make your base appear too difficult to be worth raiding.
