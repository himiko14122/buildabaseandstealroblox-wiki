Welcome to the comprehensive **Build a Base and Steal beginner guide**! This detailed tutorial will help you understand all game mechanics and get started on your journey to building the ultimate base and stealing rare pets from other players.

## 🎮 What is Build a Base and Steal?

Build a Base and Steal is a **Roblox simulation tycoon game** that combines three core gameplay elements:

### The Core Gameplay Loop
1. **Roll for Pets** - Use currency to obtain rare pets that generate income

2. **Earn Cash** - Pets generate passive income (even when you're offline!)

3. **Build and Protect** - Design and fortify a base to safeguard your collection

4. **Raid with Gear** - Use equipment to steal pets and resources from other players

### Why This Game is Unique
- **True Offline Income**: Your pets keep earning cash while you sleep, work, or go to school

- **Strategic Depth**: Balance between offense (raiding) and defense (base building)

- **Player Economy**: Trade pets and items with other players

- **Regular Updates**: New content, pets, and features added frequently

## 📚 Core Game Mechanics Explained

### Pet Rolling System

#### How Rolling Works
- **Currency Cost**: Each roll costs a specific amount of in-game cash

- **Rarity Tiers**: Pets come in different rarities (Common to Legendary)

- **Probability System**: Rarer pets have lower drop rates

- **Random Generation**: Each roll is independent with calculated probabilities

#### Pet Rarities and Values
- **Common Pets** (White): Basic income generation, easy to obtain

- **Uncommon Pets** (Green): 1.5x income rate, slightly better stats

- **Rare Pets** (Blue): 2x income rate, valuable for trading

- **Epic Pets** (Purple): 3x income rate, highly sought after

- **Legendary Pets** (Orange): 5x income rate, extremely valuable

#### Rolling Strategy for Beginners
- **Start Small**: Don't spend all your currency at once

- **Consistent Rolling**: Roll regularly rather than in large bursts

- **Event Timing**: Wait for events with boosted drop rates

- **Budget Planning**: Set aside currency for rolling vs. building

### Income Generation System

#### How Pets Earn Money
- **Passive Income**: Pets automatically generate cash over time

- **Rarity Multipliers**: Higher rarity pets earn more money

- **Collection Bonuses**: Special bonuses for complete pet sets

- **Offline Earnings**: Income continues even when you're not playing

#### Income Calculation
```
Total Income = Σ(Pet Base Rate × Rarity Multiplier × Time)
```

#### Maximizing Your Income
- **Focus on Rarity**: Prioritize higher rarity pets

- **Build Collections**: Complete sets for bonus multipliers

- **Regular Collection**: Don't let income accumulate too long

- **Strategic Investment**: Balance spending between rolling and saving

### Base Building Fundamentals

#### Base Design Principles
- **Layered Defense**: Multiple levels of protection

- **Core Protection**: Most valuable items in the center

- **Access Control**: Limit entry points to your base

- **Expandability**: Plan for future expansion

#### Building Materials
- **Basic Walls**: Standard protection, cost-effective

- **Reinforced Walls**: Stronger defense, higher cost

- **Special Materials**: Unique properties and bonuses

- **Decorative Elements**: Visual customization (no gameplay effect)

#### Defense Planning
- **Perimeter Security**: Outer layer to detect intruders

- **Inner Defense**: Stronger protection near valuable items

- **Trap Placement**: Strategic positioning of defensive traps

- **Escape Routes**: Plan for worst-case scenarios

### Raiding Mechanics

#### Understanding Raiding
- **Offensive Gameplay**: Attacking other players' bases

- **Risk vs. Reward**: Potential for high value but with costs

- **Equipment Requirements**: Need proper gear for successful raids

- **Target Selection**: Choose bases with favorable risk-reward ratios

#### Raid Preparation
- **Equipment Check**: Ensure you have the right tools

- **Intelligence Gathering**: Scout target bases before attacking

- **Cost Analysis**: Calculate potential profit vs. raid cost

- **Escape Planning**: Have a withdrawal strategy

#### Raid Execution
- **Approach**: Methodically breach outer defenses

- **Adaptation**: Adjust tactics based on encountered defenses

- **Target Acquisition**: Focus on high-value pets and resources

- **Extraction**: Exit efficiently with your loot
