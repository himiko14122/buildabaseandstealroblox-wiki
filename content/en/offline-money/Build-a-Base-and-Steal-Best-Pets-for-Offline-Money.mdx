## The Income Math You Need to Know

Before diving into the tier list, it is important to understand the income formula at a deeper level. The base rate per pet, the level multiplier, and the collection bonus all stack multiplicatively, which means a fully leveled legendary pet inside a completed legendary family can generate 30x to 50x the income of an unleveled common pet sitting outside any family. That spread is wide enough that pet selection genuinely matters, and a player who has spent the same number of rolls on smart picks will always out-earn a player who has rolled randomly.

The level multiplier is the part most beginners underweight. Every ten levels adds 25 percent to a pet's base rate, and there is no cap on pet level. A maxed legendary will produce several times its unleveled output, which means that if you have a limited supply of upgrade tokens, the legendary should always get them first. The same logic applies in reverse: a maxed common pet will never match an unleveled legendary, so do not waste tokens trying to make a common "competitive."

### S-Tier Pets for Offline Money

The S-Tier consists of the three or four pets in Build a Base and Steal that produce the highest income per storage slot, after level and family bonuses are factored in. These are the pets you should never trade away, even if you get a generous offer.

**Celestial Dragon** sits at the top of the list. Its base rate of 22 cash per second is the highest in the game, and its family bonus gives a 1.5x multiplier to every other dragon in your collection. A player with a maxed Celestial Dragon inside a completed Dragon family will see their dragon income line item account for 40 percent or more of their total offline cash, even with a full roster of other pets.

**Void Phoenix** is the second S-Tier pick. It generates 20 cash per second at base and contributes to the Mythic Avians family, which is one of the easiest mythic families to complete because most of the supporting pets have generous drop rates. A completed Mythic Avians family gives a 1.4x multiplier to every member, which compounds nicely with the phoenix's already-strong base rate.

**Astral Wolf** rounds out the S-Tier. It is the most consistent of the top three because the Wolf family has six members, all of which are relatively common, and the family bonus is a flat 1.6x, the largest in the game. Players who focus their early rolling budget on completing the Wolf family end up with one of the strongest offline income setups in the mid-game.

### A-Tier Pets for Offline Money

The A-Tier is where most active players will spend the majority of their pet slots. A-Tier pets are not the absolute best per pet, but they are widely available, easy to level, and have strong family bonuses. A roster of 20 to 30 A-Tier pets will out-earn a roster of 5 S-Tier pets and 25 commons, because the A-Tier pets bring family bonuses that the commons do not.

**Frost Tiger** is the headline A-Tier pick. It generates 12 cash per second, sits in a family of 4, and the family bonus adds 1.3x. A completed Frost Tiger family is a force multiplier for any roster. **Ember Cub** is the second A-Tier pick, with similar numbers and a stronger visual identity. **Storm Hawk** rounds out the trio, prized for its synergy with the Avians family that overlaps with the Void Phoenix.

These three families are the workhorses of the mid-game. Most experienced players aim to complete at least one of them within their first two weeks of play, and the income boost from that single completion often doubles their offline rate overnight.

### B-Tier Pets for Offline Money

The B-Tier is the "good but not great" category. B-Tier pets are worth keeping if you roll them naturally or pick them up in trades, but they are not worth specifically chasing. Their family bonuses are smaller (1.1x to 1.2x) and their base rates hover around 6 to 8 cash per second. They will not win you any income leaderboards, but they fill out a roster nicely once you have your S-Tier and A-Tier pieces in place.

**Iron Pup**, **Meadow Fawn**, and **Tide Otter** are typical B-Tier examples. Each has a small family and a modest bonus. New players often make the mistake of dumping upgrade tokens into B-Tier pets because they are excited to have a "maxed" pet, but the income gain is small. Save the tokens for higher tiers.

## Pets to Avoid Keeping

Not every pet deserves a storage slot. Some pets are objectively weak for offline money, and keeping them actively reduces your income because they take up space that could go to better pets. **Common duplicates** are the most common offender. The third copy of a common you already own contributes nothing to any family bonus and produces the same income as the first copy. Sell or trade duplicates as soon as you notice them.

**Pet skins** with no rarity tier are another trap. Some limited-time event pets look impressive but generate the same base rate as a regular common. They are collectibles, not income generators. Keep them in a separate display area if you want the aesthetic, but do not let them occupy slots in your main income line-up.

## Leveling Priority for Maximum Offline Cash

Once you have a strong roster, leveling order matters. The standard priority is: max your S-Tier pets first, then your A-Tier pets, then your B-Tier pets, and only level commons if you have surplus tokens and a full storage of better options. Most players find that maxing even one S-Tier pet to level 100 produces a larger income boost than maxing 10 commons to the same level.

The same logic applies to family completion. Completing a family grants a permanent, all-pets-included multiplier, so it is almost always worth finishing a family before chasing the next pet. A finished family of 4 average pets outperforms an unfinished family of 3 strong pets by a wide margin, because the family bonus is the larger of the two factors.
