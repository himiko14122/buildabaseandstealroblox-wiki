## The Calculator Inputs

The Build a Base and Steal AFK calculator requires five inputs. First, the total effective pet rate of your roster, which is the sum of every pet's individual rate-per-second after all happiness and aura bonuses. Second, the planned duration of your absence in real hours. Third, the active event bonus, expressed as a multiplier (1.0 if no event is active). Fourth, the number of auras in your base and their coverage percentage. Fifth, the server mood multiplier, which is 1.0 in a stable server and 0.85-0.95 during a post-raid dip.

Most of these inputs are visible directly in the game's inspection screens. The total pet rate is the sum of every pet's "effective rate" number. The planned absence is whatever you choose. The event bonus is shown on the event banner. The aura coverage can be estimated by counting the pets within range of at just performed a successful raid.

### The Master Formula

The simplified formula reads: `Offline Income = Total Pet Rate × Absence Seconds × Event Bonus × Aura Coverage × Server Mood × (1 - Diminishing Returns Penalty)`. The diminishing returns penalty is the most complex term to calculate, because it depends on the number of pets in your roster. For a 20-pet roster, the penalty is roughly 0.7. For a 10-pet roster, it is roughly 0.85. For a 30-pet roster, it is roughly 0.55. The penalty applies multiplicatively, so a higher penalty means a larger reduction in the final income.

For a quick estimate, you can use the shortcut: a 20-pet roster with full auras, a 2x event, and a stable server mood produces roughly 0.7 × 2.0 = 1.4x the income of the same roster with no event, no auras, and a neutral server mood. The cap further limits accumulation to 24 hours of effective production time, so a planned absence of more than 24 hours does not increase your total income unless you have the premium 72-hour cap.

### The Planning Worksheet

For a hands-on approach, the worksheet below is what most serious Build a Base and Steal players use to plan their base. Step one: list every pet in your roster with its base rate. Step two: multiply each pet by its current happiness to get its effective rate. Step three: sum all effective rates. Step four: apply the diminishing-returns penalty based on total pet count. For ongoing optimization, run this worksheet once per week. Compare your predicted income to your actual collected income. ## Common Calculator Mistakes

The most common mistake is forgetting the diminishing returns penalty. A player who sums their effective pet rates and assumes the sum is the final number will overestimate their income by 15-50%, depending on roster size. Always apply the penalty, even if you are not the wrong absence duration. Real hours are not the same as effective pet production hours during events, and the cap is calculated in effective time, not real time. A 24-hour absence during a 2x event hits the cap in 12 hours of real time.

The third most common mistake is assuming the server mood is always 1.0. In a busy server, the mood fluctuates constantly, and a long absence during a raid-heavy period can reduce income by 10-15% even without any direct action on your part. Players who want the most accurate estimates should check the server mood in the recent-activity feed before logging off. If the mood is below 1.0, plan to log in sooner than you otherwise would, because every additional hour of low-mood accumulation is wasted income.
