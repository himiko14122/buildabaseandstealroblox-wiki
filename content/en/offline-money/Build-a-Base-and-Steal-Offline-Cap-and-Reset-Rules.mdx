## The 8-Hour Default Cap

Every Build a Base and Steal account starts with an 8-hour offline cap. This cap determines the maximum number of hours of offline income you can accumulate in a single absence. If you log off for 8 hours or less, you will earn income for the full duration of your absence. If you log off for more than 8 hours, you will only earn for the first 8 hours, and any additional time will not produce cash.

The cap is calculated using server time, not local time. The server logs the moment your client disconnects and the moment you reconnect, and the difference is the "offline duration" that the cap is applied to. Network lag, brief disconnects, and reconnects are all handled gracefully: the system waits for a stable reconnection before starting the next offline clock, so a momentary blip will not accidentally consume your offline window.

### When the Clock Starts

The clock starts the moment internet connection, or being kicked by anti-cheat. It does not include tabbing out, minimizing the window, or going idle in the lobby. Idle players who stay in the game but stop moving are still considered "online" by the system and continue to earn at the higher online rate.

This is an important distinction. Some players think that alt-tabbing for an hour counts as offline, but it does not. As long as the game is the active process on your client, the offline clock is paused. The trade-off is that you are also not earning at the maximum online rate, because being idle lowers your effective activity score, which affects certain event bonuses. For pure offline income optimization, fully closing the client is best.

A reset, in the context of offline money, is any event that forces the offline clock to restart from zero maintenance, all offline timers are wiped, and players reconnect to a fresh server with no accumulated offline cash. Maintenance typically happens on Sunday nights, and the developers usually announce the window a few days in advance. Plan your longest absences around maintenance windows, not across them, or you risk losing the entire offline window.

This is one of the few scenarios where being offline is actually safer than being online, because the merge can sometimes drop active players into a different instance and confuse the income tracking briefly.
