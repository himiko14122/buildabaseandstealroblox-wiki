The rebirth and prestige system in Build a Base and Steal is the long-term progression mechanic that separates casual players from committed ones. Rebirthing resets most of your progress in exchange for a permanent multiplier that applies to all future income, including offline income. The decision of when to rebirth — and what to preserve through the reset — is one full detail, with specific focus on how it interacts with offline money and how to time your rebirths for maximum compounding.

## How Rebirth Works

Rebirthing in Build a Base and Steal is a one-way action that resets your base, your cash, your pet roster (with exceptions), and your gear, but grants a permanent prestige multiplier that applies to all future cash generation. The prestige multiplier starts at 1.10x for the first rebirth and scales up by 0.05-0.10x per subsequent rebirth, depending on the conditions met at the time of the reset. There is no upper limit on the prestige multiplier, but each additional rebirth requires more total lifetime value to unlock, so the rate of progression slows as you climb.

The reset is not total. Build a Base and Steal preserves three categories of progress through a rebirth: your account level, your unlocked pet slots, and any pets you have marked as "favorite" before initiating the reset. The favorite system lets you protect up to five pets per rebirth, and these pets retain their full level, happiness, and effectiveness. Pets that are not favorited are converted into a small cash bonus based on their rarity, which is added to the post-rebirth starting cash.

### The Prestige Multi earns 50% more cash from every source, including base pets, offline accumulation, raid rewards, and event bonuses. The multiplier is calculated as `1 + (Rebirth Count × Per-Rebirth Bonus × Bonus Conditions)`, where bonus conditions include things like reaching a specific pet count, holding a mythic at the time of reset, and having no losses during the run.

A player who has completed ten rebirths with average bonus conditions has a prestige multiplier around 1.5x. A player who has completed twenty rebirths with optimal conditions can reach 2.0x or higher. Beyond 2.0x, the per-rebirth bonus contribution becomes small relative to the existing multiplier, and the marginal value of each additional rebirth decreases. The optimal stopping point for most players is somewhere between 1.75x and 2.5x, depending on how much they enjoy the early-game loop.

### Preserving Your Best Pets

The favorite system is the single most important tool for protecting your progress through a rebirth. Before initiating a reset, mark your five highest-rarity pets as favorites. These pets carry over with their full effectiveness, which means your post-rebirth base starts with a serious head start. The five-pet limit is tight, and choosing which pets to protect is a strategic decision that depends on your long-term goals.

For most players, the optimal five favorites are the five highest-rarity pets in the current roster, regardless of other factors. Rarity is the largest single determinant of pet effectiveness, and a post-rebirth mythic is worth more than three or four post-rebirth legendaries. The exception is when that case, preserving that legendary over a slightly-higher-rarity alternative can be correct.

## When to Rebirth

The decision of when to rebirth is a balance between the immediate value of your current base and the long-term value of the prestige multiplier. Rebirthing too early means sacrificing a strong base for a small multiplier. Rebirthing too late means missing the compounding benefit of the multiplier during a period when your income would have been highest. The optimal timing is somewhere in the middle, and it depends on your current prestige level, your roster strength, and the upcoming events.

A common heuristic is to rebirth when your current base has plateaued. If you have stopped adding pets because the diminishing-returns curve is biting, if you have optimized your auras and happiness and there is no more room for improvement, and if your daily income has been flat for a week, it is probably time to rebirth. The plateau signals that you have extracted everything you can from the current configuration, and a reset will let you rebuild with the prestige multiplier compounding your income.

### The Prestige Plateau

Every Build a Base and Steal player eventually hits a prestige plateau, where additional rebirths produce diminishing marginal benefit. The plateau typically occurs around a 2.5-3.0x multiplier, depending on the player's willingness to grind for optimal bonus conditions. Past the plateau, the per-rebirth bonus is small enough that the reset cost outweighs the long-term gain, and the optimal strategy shifts from aggressive rebirth cycling to base optimization within the current prestige level.

The plateau is not a dead end. It is a transition point where the game shifts from a progression-focused loop to an optimization-focused loop.
