# First Pet Rolling Guide

Pet rolling is the heart and soul of Build a Base and Steal. Understanding how to approach your first rolling sessions can make the difference between a thriving pet collection and a depleted wallet. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about making smart, strategic rolling decisions from your very first roll.

## Understanding the Pet Rolling System

Before you spend your first coin on rolling, it's essential to understand how the system works. The pet rolling mechanics in Build a Base and Steal are built around probability tiers, with each rarity category having specific drop rates and income potential.

### Rarity Tiers Explained

The game features several distinct rarity tiers, each with increasing rarity and value:

**Common Pets:** These make up about 60% of all rolls. They have basic income generation and are the foundation of your early game economy. While not exciting, they're essential for building a stable income stream.

**Uncommon Pets:** Approximately 25% of rolls result in uncommon pets. These offer 2-3x the income of common pets and often have slight bonuses to specific income types.

**Rare Pets:** At roughly 10% drop rate, rare pets start becoming noticeable in your collection. They provide 5-8x the income of commons and may have special abilities that enhance your overall earnings.

**Epic Pets:** With only 4% drop rate, epic pets are significant achievements. They generate 15-25x common pet income and often come with powerful passive abilities.

**Legendary Pets:** The crown jewels at just 1% drop rate. These pets generate massive income (50-100x common pets) and have game-changing abilities that can transform your entire strategy.

### Probability Mathematics

Understanding the math behind rolling can help you make informed decisions:

**Expected Value Calculation:** Each roll has an expected value based on the probability of each tier multiplied by that tier's income potential. This helps you determine if a rolling session is likely to be profitable.

**Variance and Risk Management:** Even with good expected value, variance means you can have streaks of good or bad luck. Managing this variance through budget control is crucial for long-term success.

**Compound Probability:** The chance of rolling specific combinations or sequences follows compound probability rules. This becomes important when you're trying to complete sets or achieve specific outcomes.

## Your First Rolling Budget

Establishing a proper rolling budget is perhaps the most critical skill for new players. Many beginners make the mistake of spending all their money on rolling without considering the consequences.

### The 30% Rule

A proven strategy for new players is the **30% Rule**: never spend more than 30% of your total available funds on any single rolling session. This ensures you always have reserves for base maintenance, emergencies, and other important investments.

For example, if you have 10,000 coins:
- Maximum rolling budget: 3,000 coins
- Reserve for other purposes: 7,000 coins

This conservative approach might seem limiting, but it protects you from catastrophic losses due to bad luck.

### Budget Allocation Strategies

Different players prefer different budget allocation strategies:

**Conservative Approach:** 20% rolling, 40% base defense, 40% savings/investment
- **Pros:** Maximum safety, steady growth
- **Cons:** Slower pet collection progression

**Balanced Approach:** 30% rolling, 35% base defense, 35% savings/investment
- **Pros:** Good balance of growth and safety
- **Cons:** Moderate risk exposure

**Aggressive Approach:** 40% rolling, 30% base defense, 30% savings/investment
- **Pros:** Faster pet collection, higher potential rewards
- **Cons:** Higher risk of significant losses

### Emergency Fund Management

Always maintain an emergency fund equal to at least 50% of your rolling budget. This fund covers unexpected expenses like:
- Base repairs after failed raids
- Sudden opportunities (limited-time events, special promotions)
- Emergency defensive upgrades when under threat

## First Rolling Session Strategy

Your approach to the first rolling session can set the tone for your entire early game experience.

### Pre-Rolling Preparation

Before you start rolling, ensure you have:
1. **Adequate Storage:** Make sure you have space in your base for new pets
2. **Base Defense:** Your base should provide basic protection for new acquisitions
3. **Clear Goals:** Know what you're hoping to achieve (specific pets, income targets, etc.)
4. **Time Allocation:** Set aside enough time to properly manage new acquisitions

### The "Warm-Up" Strategy

Start with smaller rolling sessions to get a feel for the system:

**Session 1:** 10% of your rolling budget
- **Goal:** Acquire 5-10 common pets for baseline income
- **Strategy:** Focus on quantity over quality, establish income streams

**Session 2:** 20% of your rolling budget
- **Goal:** Acquire 3-5 uncommon pets and aim for your first rare
- **Strategy:** Increase stakes slightly, start building variety

**Session 3:** 30% of your rolling budget
- **Goal:** Target specific rare pets, begin epic hunting
- **Strategy:** Strategic rolling focused on filling collection gaps

### Reading the Patterns

As you roll more, you'll start to notice patterns in the RNG (random number generation):

**Hot Streaks:** Periods where you seem to get better results than expected
- **Strategy:** Consider increasing your rolling budget slightly during hot streaks, but maintain discipline

**Cold Streaks:** Periods with below-average results
- **Strategy:** Reduce rolling activity, focus on other aspects of the game, wait for patterns to shift

**Pattern Cycles:** Many players believe the game has inherent cycles in its RNG
- **Strategy:** Track your results over time to identify any personal patterns

## Evaluating Your Rolls

Not all pets are created equal, and knowing how to evaluate the results of your rolling sessions is crucial for long-term success.

### Income Potential Assessment

When you acquire a new pet, evaluate its income potential across several dimensions:

**Base Income Rate:** How much does the pet generate per hour?
- **Comparison:** Is this better than your current average?
- **Scaling:** How does this scale with upgrades?

**Income Multiplier:** Does the pet have any passive income bonuses?
- **Global Effects:** Does it boost all pets or specific types?
- **Stacking:** How does it interact with other multipliers?

**Special Abilities:** Unique properties that can enhance your overall strategy
- **Synergy Potential:** How well does it work with your existing pets?
- **Situational Value:** When is this ability most useful?

### Collection Value Considerations

Beyond immediate income, consider the pet's value to your overall collection:

**Set Completion:** Does this pet help complete a collection set?
- **Set Bonuses:** Many games provide bonuses for completing themed sets
- **Display Value:** Some pets have prestige or display value

**Trading Potential:** How desirable is this pet for other players?
- **Demand Level:** Is this pet sought after in the community?
- **Rarity vs. Utility:** Balance between rarity and actual usefulness

**Upgrade Path:** Does this pet have good upgrade potential?
- **Growth Ceiling:** How much can this pet improve with investment?
- **Upgrade Cost:** Is the investment worth the potential returns?

## Advanced Rolling Techniques

Once you've mastered the basics, you can start employing more sophisticated rolling strategies.

### Targeted Rolling

Instead of random rolling, focus on specific goals:

**Rarity Targeting:** Focus your rolling efforts on acquiring pets of a specific rarity tier
- **Timing:** Roll when you have maximum budget for that tier
- **Quantity:** Plan for multiple attempts to account for probability

**Set Completion Rolling:** Roll specifically to complete collection sets
- **Priority:** Focus on missing pieces rather than random acquisitions
- **Efficiency:** Stop rolling once set is complete, move to next goal

**Income Optimization Rolling:** Roll specifically to maximize income generation
- **ROI Focus:** Prioritize pets with the best income-to-cost ratios
- **Passive Income:** Target pets with strong passive abilities

### Timing Strategies

The timing of your rolling sessions can impact your results:

**Peak Hours Rolling:** Some players believe rolling during peak server hours yields better results
- **Community Activity:** Higher player activity might influence RNG
- **Event Timing:** Time rolling around in-game events for potential bonuses

**Off-Hours Rolling:** Others prefer rolling during quieter periods
- **Less Competition:** Fewer players rolling might mean better personal odds
- **Focus:** Better concentration and decision-making

**Update Cycles:** Time rolling around game updates
- **New Content:** Updates often introduce new pets with temporary boosted rates
- **Balance Changes:** Patch notes might indicate favorable rolling conditions

## Common Rolling Mistakes to Avoid

Even experienced players can fall into these common traps:

### Chase Losses Syndrome

The urge to keep rolling after a bad session to "make up" for losses
- **Problem:** Emotional decision-making leads to poor strategic choices
- **Solution:** Set strict rolling limits and stick to them regardless of outcomes

### Ignoring Opportunity Cost

Focusing only on rolling while neglecting other important game aspects
- **Problem:** Missing out on base upgrades, raiding opportunities, and other growth areas
- **Solution:** Maintain balanced allocation across all game systems

### Overvaluing Rarity

Choosing pets based on rarity alone rather than actual utility
- **Problem:** Rare pets with poor abilities may be less valuable than uncommon pets with great abilities
- **Solution:** Evaluate pets holistically, considering multiple factors

### Underestimating Maintenance

Failing to account for the ongoing costs of pet ownership
- **Problem:** Pets require ongoing investment for upgrades, maintenance, and protection
- **Solution:** Include long-term costs in your rolling budget calculations

- Personal hot and cold streaks
- Time of day correlations
- Relationship between game events and rolling outcomes

### Using This Data

Regularly review your rolling data to:
- Identify your most successful strategies
- Recognize and avoid problematic patterns
- Optimize your budget allocation
- Plan future rolling sessions more effectively
