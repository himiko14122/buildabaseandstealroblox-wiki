# Getting Started Guide

Welcome to Build a Base and Steal! This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know as a new player. Whether you're completely new to tycoon games or just starting with this Roblox experience, we'll cover all the essential mechanics, strategies, and tips to get you started on the right foot.

## Understanding the Core Gameplay Loop

Build a Base and Steal revolves around three main pillars: **pet rolling**, **base building**, and **raiding**. Understanding how these mechanics work together is crucial for your success in the game.

The fundamental loop works like this: You roll for pets that generate passive income even when you're offline. You use that income to build and upgrade your base to protect your valuable pets. Then, you can raid other players' bases to steal their pets and treasures, accelerating your progress. It's a delicate balance between building your empire and aggressively expanding through conquest.

### Pet Rolling Mechanics

Pet rolling is the primary way to acquire income-generating assets in the game. Each pet comes with different rarity levels, income multipliers, and special abilities. When you start, you'll have basic access to common and uncommon pets, but as you progress, you'll unlock chances to roll for rarer and more valuable specimens.

The rolling system uses a probability-based mechanic where rarer pets have lower drop rates but significantly higher income potential. Understanding these odds and managing your rolling budget is one of the most important skills you'll develop as a player.

### Base Building Fundamentals

Your base serves as both your storage facility and your defensive fortress. The placement and design of your base directly impacts how well you can protect your valuable pets from other players' raids. Good base design involves creating efficient storage layouts while incorporating defensive structures that make raiding difficult for potential attackers.

As you progress, you'll unlock new building materials, defensive structures, and layout options that allow for increasingly sophisticated base designs.

### Raiding System

Raiding is the aggressive side of the game where you can significantly accelerate your progress by stealing from other players. Successful raids can net you valuable pets, resources, and income multipliers. However, raiding also carries risks, as failed raids can result in losses and damage to your own base and gear.

## Your First Steps in the Game

When you first spawn into Build a Base and Steal, you'll want to focus on establishing a solid foundation before expanding into more complex strategies.

### Initial Tutorial and Setup

The game provides a comprehensive tutorial that walks you through the basic mechanics. Pay close attention to this tutorial, as it covers essential controls, UI elements, and fundamental gameplay concepts that you'll use throughout your entire playthrough.

After completing the tutorial, take some time to familiarize yourself with the game interface. Understand where your inventory is located, how to access the rolling system, and where to find your base building controls. This familiarity will save you countless hours of frustration later.

### Starting Capital Management

You begin the game with a small amount of starting capital. How you choose to spend this initial money can significantly impact your early game progress. Many new players make the mistake of immediately spending all their money on pet rolling without establishing a proper base first.

**Recommended Early Spending Strategy:**
- Allocate 40% of your starting funds to basic base construction
- Use 30% for initial pet rolling to establish income streams
- Keep 30% as a reserve for emergency repairs and unexpected opportunities

This balanced approach ensures you have both income generation and protection from the start.

### First Pet Selection

Your first pets will likely be common or uncommon varieties, but don't underestimate their importance. Even basic pets can generate meaningful income when managed properly. Focus on acquiring a diverse portfolio of pets rather than chasing that one ultra-rare specimen immediately.

Different pets have different income generation patterns - some produce steady, consistent income while others generate larger amounts less frequently. Understanding these patterns will help you plan your rolling strategy and budget management.

## Building Your First Base

Your first base doesn't need to be an impenetrable fortress, but it should provide basic protection for your valuable pets while allowing for efficient expansion.

### Basic Layout Principles

Start with a simple, rectangular layout that's easy to expand upon. Place your most valuable pets in the center of your base, surrounded by progressively less valuable assets. This concentric defense approach makes it harder for raiders to reach your best loot.

Ensure you leave adequate space between defensive structures and storage areas. This spacing prevents raiders from targeting multiple assets simultaneously and gives your defenses more time to respond to attacks.

### Essential Defensive Structures

Even in the early game, certain defensive structures are worth their weight in gold:

**Walls and Barriers:** These are your first line of defense. Basic wooden walls provide minimal protection but are cost-effective for new players. Upgrade to stronger materials as your budget allows.

**Storage Units:** Proper storage is crucial for protecting your pets and resources. Start with basic storage chests and upgrade to more secure options as you progress.

**Early Warning Systems:** Simple alarm systems can alert you to potential raids, giving you time to prepare your defenses or move valuable assets to safety.

## Understanding Income Generation

The passive income system is what makes Build a Base and Steal unique among Roblox tycoon games. Understanding how to maximize this income is key to your success.

### Pet Income Mechanics

Each pet generates income based on its rarity tier, level, and any special abilities it possesses. The income generation happens continuously, even when you're offline, making strategic pet selection and placement crucial for maximizing your earnings.

Higher rarity pets generate more income but also cost more to acquire and maintain. The goal is to find the optimal balance between acquisition cost and income generation potential.

### Offline Income Optimization

One of the most powerful features of Build a Base and Steal is its offline income system. Your pets continue generating income even when you're not playing, allowing for meaningful progress even with limited playtime.

To maximize offline income:
- Ensure your pets are properly placed and functioning before logging off
- Upgrade pets with the best income-to-cost ratios first
- Time your pet upgrades to maximize their offline productivity

### Income Reinvestment Strategy

Deciding how to reinvest your income is one of the most important strategic decisions you'll make. Generally, you'll want to follow a progression of:

1. **Safety First:** Invest in base defenses to protect your existing assets
2. **Income Expansion:** Roll for new pets and upgrade existing ones
3. **Aggressive Growth:** Save for high-value raids and rare pet acquisitions

This balanced approach ensures steady growth while protecting your investments.

## Common Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

Many new players make predictable mistakes that can significantly slow their progress. Learning from these common errors will help you advance faster:

### Overspending on Rolling

The temptation to continuously roll for pets can be overwhelming, especially when you're chasing that one rare specimen. However, depleting your entire budget on rolling leaves you vulnerable to raids and unable to invest in necessary upgrades.

**Solution:** Set a rolling budget and stick to it. Never spend more than 30-40% of your total income on rolling at any given time.

### Neglecting Base Defense

Some players focus so heavily on acquiring pets that they neglect their base defenses, making them easy targets for raiders. This mistake can result in losing your valuable investments to aggressive players.

**Solution:** Always maintain a minimum defensive investment equal to at least 25% of your total asset value.

### Poor Pet Placement

Randomly placing pets throughout your base might seem convenient, but it makes defense much more difficult and can reduce your overall income efficiency.

**Solution:** Plan your pet placement strategically, grouping pets by value and creating defensive layers around your most valuable assets.
